SPELL MASTER DOCUMENT
=====================
Comprehensive reference for all symbols, solo spells, combo spells, and weapon summons.
This is a living design doc for planning new interactions.
All combo descriptions use kanji symbol references.

================================================================================
PART 1: SYMBOL REGISTRY
================================================================================
Each symbol can be cast alone (solo spell) or combined with one other symbol (combo).
Symbols are learned from rune stones or NPCs in the world.

DESIGN DECISIONS:
- 闇 coexists with 空. 闇 = corruptive darkness, 空 = neutral emptiness.
- 炎 is an upgraded 火. Costs more, does more.
- 切 is a combo modifier (dud alone). Used with 風、水 etc. for elemental cuts.
- 動 has been REMOVED from the design.
- 石 sits alongside 土. 石 is for offensive physical magic.
- 風 is "wind" — cutting wind, not gentle air.

PART 5: PROJECTILE SPELLS
================================================================================
Spells that use the projectile system (like 弓 arrows but magic-based).
Fire toward the mouse cursor at arbitrary angles.

Currently projectile-based:
- 弓 arrows (and all bow enchantments)

Candidates for future projectile conversion:
- 土＋力 Boulder Throw — could be a slow, heavy projectile
- 水＋力 Water Jet — could be a fast water bolt
- 雷＋力 (future) — lightning bolt projectile
- 切 combos — could produce slashing projectile arcs

Projectile properties to define per spell:
- Speed (tiles/sec)           - Damage + type
- Hitbox size (sub-tile)      - Max range (tiles)
- Status effects on hit       - Piercing vs stopping on hit
- AoE on impact?              - Mana cost / charge time

PART 6: STATUS EFFECTS REFERENCE
================================================================================

ELEMENTAL:
- burning       Damage over time (fire)
- wet           Vulnerable to electric/ice, extinguishes fire
- frozen        Cannot move
- electrified   Shock damage, chains through water/metal
- chilled       Slowed, precursor to frozen
- heated        Hot to touch, precursor to burning

CONDITION:
- poisoned      Damage over time
- stunned       Cannot act
- slowed        Reduced move speed (1.5x cooldown)
- hastened      Increased move speed (0.5x cooldown)
- muddy         Slowed by mud (1.4x cooldown)
- obscured      Vision impaired (fog overlay on screen)

SPECIAL:
- invisible     Hidden
- invulnerable  Cannot take damage
- silenced      Cannot cast magic

DARK/SPIRIT:
- feared        From dark+spirit
- cursed        Dark affliction
- withered      Dark damage to organic
- decaying      Dark damage to plants / weakening over time
- weakened      Reduced effectiveness
- tainted       Corrupted (dark+liquid)

LIGHT/GROWTH:
- blessed       Light protection, removes dark statuses
- growing       Plant growth from water

PART 7: ELEMENTAL REACTION SYSTEM REFERENCE
================================================================================
The reaction system (src/reactions/) handles element+element and element+attribute
interactions automatically when spells hit targets.

Elements (active forces): Fire, Water, Ice, Electric, Wind, Earth, Light, Dark, Spirit
Attributes (inherent tags): Organic, Plant, Metal, Liquid, Stone

Key reactions already implemented — see src/reactions/default_reactions.py for full list.
Any new elements or attributes from new symbols will need reactions registered.

New element mappings needed:
- 電 and 雷 both map to Element.ELECTRIC (different intensities)
- 闇 maps to Element.DARK (distinct from 空 which maps to a "void" element — may need new Element.VOID)
- 炎 maps to Element.FIRE (higher intensity variant)
- 切 maps to no element (physical modifier)
- 石 maps to Element.EARTH (physical variant)

PART 8: DESIGN NOTES / DECISIONS LOG
================================================================================

DECIDED:
- 闇 coexists with 空. 闇 = corruptive darkness, 空 = neutral emptiness.
- 炎 is an upgraded 火. Costs more, does more.
- 切 is a combo modifier for elemental cuts. Dud alone.
- 動 has been CUT from the design entirely.
- 石 sits alongside 土 for offensive physical magic. Dud alone.
- 風 renamed conceptually to "Wind" — cutting wind.
- 電 added as basic electric (alongside 雷 as upgraded electric).
- Weapon enchantments drain mana per strike. No mana = no elemental effect.
- Cross-weapon combos (刀＋斧, 刀＋弓, etc.) are null/dud.
- All weapon summon costs increased (刀=40, 斧=40, 大刀=50, 大斧=60, 弓=60).
- 弓 costs 10 mp/shot (up from 5).
