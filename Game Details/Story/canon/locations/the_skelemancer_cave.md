# The Skelemancer's Cave

## Locked Endpoints

- A cave system in a remote wilderness area.
- The surrounding environment feels wrong — barren, quiet, stripped of ambient life.
- Animals avoid this place. The land isn't corrupted in the hollowing sense — it's just dead.
- Not drained in the way a hollowed area is drained. Just absent. Ambient life that generates mana doesn't exist here.
- This is a site of concentrated mass death from the collapse (~25 years ago). Dozens or hundreds died here.
- Ambient mana — always present in the world — pooled at this site over decades, organizing around the concentrated grief and terror. The Skelemancer formed passively, without any human casting. The despair was the signal; the bones were the available material.
- The Skelemancer has been growing here for years, gathering bones, expanding — long before anyone knew magic existed.

## Established Details

- Bone piles, arrangements suggesting intelligence — walls, structures, crude architecture.
- Evidence of the fall's death toll — dozens or hundreds of people absorbed over time.
- Signs of previous investigation parties who didn't survive.
- No insects, no moss, no fungi. Even decay organisms avoid this place.
