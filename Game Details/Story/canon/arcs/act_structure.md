# Story Act Structure

## Prologue / Tutorial — "The Village"
- Player wakes in the village. Daily life. Brother teaches basic symbols (tutorial + relationship).
- Interactions with parents and childhood friend. These must be specific people.
- Small tasks using basic magic. Magic is still new — people are experimenting, adapting, figuring out what works. The player has learned a few symbols but mastery is far off.
- Duration: ~20-30 minutes.
- **The attack.** Coordinated. Brother saves the player's life. Parents die. Friend dies. Village torn but standing.
- **The departure.** Brother can't stay. Asks player to come. Leaves. The four compass NPCs emerge.

## Act One — "The First Steps"
- Opportunities aligned with all four compass directions.
- Early quests introduce each NPC naturally.
- Brother is already gone — NPCs mention him.
- First real exploration beyond home (or deep investment in home for community).
- Environmental mana system becomes apparent — the realization that the bar isn't "yours."
- Mentor researcher introduced through the companion researcher.
- First soul level milestone. Non-combat.

## Act Two — "The Widening World"
- **Quest fork structure.** Multiple quests available per fork, one per compass. Player can only choose one.
- Active quests may result in encountering the brother. Passive quests = brother alone.
- The brother's gradual change on active paths. Understanding corruption builds on all paths.
- By mid-Act 2, active-path player should understand hollowing enough for informed choices.
- World events unfold regardless of path. Settlements struggle. Mana scarcity matters.

- The world continues. Communities grow, struggle, adapt.
- The player's legacy defined by their choices — not explained, not judged.
