# The Home Village Community — Structure and People

## Overview

The village is fifteen families (roughly 70-90 people). Not a coincidence of number — fifteen is the maximum size that allows genuine collective decision-making and resource sharing without formal hierarchy. Below that, everyone knows everyone. Above that, you need structure and authority. The village has chosen to stay small.

The families are not interchangeable. Each one has a specific role, a history, relationships to other families, and a stake in the village's survival. Losing a quarter of that number (the attack deaths) means losing specific people who did specific things. The damage is not abstract.

---

## The Fifteen Families

### FAMILY 1: The Players' Family (THORNE & ELSA)
**Role:** Thorne — Builder. Elsa — Healer/Keeper.  
**Members:** Thorne, Elsa, the Player (surviving), the Brother (left)  
**What They Do:** Thorne builds and maintains structures. Elsa handles medical knowledge and stores. Between them, they've created the physical and health infrastructure the village depends on.  
**How They Died:** Killed in the attack, defending others. See the_parents_before_the_fall.md for full character development.  
**Impact on Village:** Their deaths leave a massive functional gap. No one else can do what Thorne did or what Elsa did. The village is now scrambling to learn how to maintain buildings and manage medicine.

### FAMILY 2: KAI & MERITH (Hunters/Scouts)
**Role:** Food acquisition and long-range awareness.  
**Members:** Kai (40s, steady), Merith (40s, sharp-eyed), their son TOREN (17, learning), VERA (Merith's sister, visiting from another settlement when attack happened)  
**What They Do:** Kai and Merith hunt. They know the valleys and mountains. They're the village's eyes on the wider world — they bring news, gather information, scout new areas. Toren is apprenticing into this role.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived the attack. Kai took injuries protecting Merith. He recovered, but it changed him. Now he's more cautious, and the village's long-range scouts are operating in fear.  
**Current Tensions:** Vera's presence complicates things. She's from a settlement three valleys over. She was visiting family when the attack happened. Now she's trapped here (roads are dangerous, winter coming). She speaks of her village differently — more hardship, less cooperation. Is that how all villages work? Or is hers failing? This creates an undercurrent of anxiety: *are we the exception?*  
**Community Path Role:** If the player pursues community, Kai and Merith become key allies in rebuilding confidence. Their willingness to scout again, to re-engage with the wider world, signals that the village isn't cursed.

### FAMILY 3: THANE (Elder Advisor)
**Role:** Memory and decision-making.  
**Members:** Thane (70s, sharp-minded), his spouse LISS (also 70s), their daughter JENNA (40s), Jenna's two children ROWAN (8) and MET (5)  
**What They Do:** Thane is the closest the village has to a leader — but he leads through consensus, not command. He remembers pre-collapse knowledge. He's studied what the elders told him. He holds the village's history and helps settle disputes. Liss is a gardener; together they manage the seed stores. Jenna is a carpenter's apprentice (trained by Thorne before his death). Her children are the youngest in the village.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived. Thane was struck in the attack but lived. Liss sheltered the children. Now Thane is aware of his mortality. The village has lost younger people — if he dies, the knowledge dies with him.  
**Current Tensions:** Thane is pushing for documenting everything he knows, but there's no one dedicated to learning it. The village is too focused on immediate survival to think about transmission. Thane feels the weight of this gap.  
**Community Path Role:** Thane becomes a mentor figure to the player. His mentorship is different from Eloth's — Thane teaches through shared history and perspective, not through research. He represents continuity.

### FAMILY 4: KESS (Healer's Apprentice/Herbalist)
**Role:** Supporting Elsa's healing work (now taking over it).  
**Members:** Kess (30s, careful, methodical), her partner RENN (30s, pragmatic), their child LINN (6, fearful after the attack)  
**What They Do:** Kess was learning healing from Elsa when the attack happened. She knew the basics but not the deep knowledge. Now she's scrambling to remember what Elsa taught, reading any notes left behind, trying to keep people alive on incomplete knowledge. Renn works with food preservation (smoking meat, storing vegetables). Together they manage food safety.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived, but Kess was traumatized by the loss of her teacher and the sudden weight of responsibility. She's not confident in her knowledge. People come to her for healing, and she's terrified of making mistakes. Renn is the steady one, keeping her grounded.  
**Current Tension:** Kess is trying to learn everything from Elsa's memory, but she didn't get formal training. She patches gaps with educated guesses. Some of her treatments work. Others don't. The village doesn't blame her, but she blames herself. She's carrying the burden of Elsa's death plus the weight of the village's health.  
**Community Path Role:** Kess becomes a collaborator if the player pursues knowledge-based healing. Together they could rebuild Elsa's medical tradition. Or Kess could become a vulnerability — if she fails at a crucial moment, it breaks community trust.

### FAMILY 5: ROAN (Smith/Craftsperson)
**Role:** Making and maintaining tools.  
**Members:** Roan (50s, pragmatic, weathered), their apprentices TAL (16) and MERIN (15)  
**What They Do:** Roan runs the village's forge. They make and repair tools, weapons, cooking implements. This sounds small, but everything the village does depends on tools. Without Roan, farming and hunting become exponentially harder. Tal and Merin are learning the trade, but they're still years from independence.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived. Roan's forge is one of the few structures left mostly intact (built with fire safety in mind). During the attack, Roan sheltered people there. This has given Roan a strange status — survivor-turned-shelter-provider. Roan is respected now in a way they weren't before.  
**Current Tensions:** The forge work is relentless. Roan is exhausted. Tools break. Weapons need maintenance. Tal and Merin are capable but not experienced. If Roan burns out, the village's physical infrastructure starts failing.  
**Community Path Role:** Roan represents the backbone of rebuilding. If the community path involves reconstruction, Roan is essential. The player might work alongside Roan, learning the trade, or help find ways to reduce the burden.

### FAMILY 6: DYER & WEAVER (Textile Workers)
**Role:** Clothing, rope, nets, textiles.  
**Members:** DYER (40s, creative), WEAVER (40s, patient), their three children SENNA (12), JOS (10), and MARA (7)  
**What They Do:** They create the textiles the village depends on. Dyer manages the color work; Weaver handles the looms. Together they produce cloth, rope, fishing nets, clothing repairs. It's meticulous, essential work that no one thinks about until they need it.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived, but their work area was partially damaged. Some looms are broken. Supplies are scattered. They're working to rebuild capacity while maintaining supply to the rest of the village.  
**Current Tensions:** The children are showing trauma responses. Mara has stopped speaking. Senna is angry. Jos is clingy. Dyer and Weaver are trying to maintain their work while supporting their children's emotional recovery. They're splitting their attention and doing both poorly.  
**Community Path Role:** If the player involves themselves in the community's daily life, Dyer and Weaver are where textile work gets done. Working alongside them is meditative and grounding. It's also where village gossip happens.

### FAMILY 7: LEXX (Fisher/Aquaculture)
**Role:** Fresh water, fish, water-based resources.  
**Members:** Lexx (60s, quiet, observant), their child PINE (25, energetic), PINE's partner RETH (25, thoughtful)  
**What They Do:** Lexx manages the village's relationship with the river. Fish traps, water irrigation, checking the spring. Lexx has an almost intuitive sense for water systems. Pine and Reth are learning, experimenting with small aquaculture projects (might work, might not). This family is less visible than hunters or builders, but the village doesn't survive without them.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived without direct casualties. But the attack's consequences are visible in the water — strange cloudiness after heavy rains suggests damaged structures upstream. Lexx is investigating, worried that something's wrong beyond the valley.  
**Current Tensions:** Lexx's quiet nature means people don't always listen. When Lexx tries to warn about potential water issues, some dismiss it as worry. Lexx is used to this, but it's wearing. Pine is more vocal and might become the face of water management going forward.  
**Community Path Role:** Water is fundamental. If the player engages with resource management, Lexx becomes important. Also, Lexx's quiet wisdom is a different kind of mentorship than Thane or Eloth.

### FAMILY 8: MIRA (Teacher/Scribe)
**Role:** Preserving knowledge, teaching children, documentation.  
**Members:** Mira (35, energetic, frustrated), their child KAEL's SIBLING (8, curious), two adopted children from other settlements (6 and 4, traumatized)  
**What They Do:** Mira runs the village's collective knowledge work. Teaching children to read/write, recording village decisions, maintaining memory through writing. This role barely existed before the collapse. Mira is making it up as they go — convinced that writing and documentation are how humanity survives the loss of technology.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived. One of the children they were teaching (Kael, the childhood friend) was killed. Mira is grieving the loss of a student and worried that without documentation, Kael will be forgotten. The two adopted children came to the village *after* the attack, seeking refuge.  
**Current Tensions:** Mira is documenting everything compulsively. Some people think this is useful. Others think Mira is obsessed and should focus on present survival. Mira is fighting to validate the work of remembering.  
**Community Path Role:** Mira represents the transmission of knowledge across time. If the player cares about preserving the village's story, Mira is a natural ally.

### FAMILY 9: SHEN (Forager/Medicinal Plants)
**Role:** Gathering wild resources, especially medicinal plants.  
**Members:** Shen (45, wandering-minded), their partner JOY (45, grounding), their child RAD (13, adventurous)  
**What They Do:** Shen knows which plants are edible, which have medicinal properties, which can be used for fiber or dye. Shen spends half the year away from the village, ranging through the mountains. Joy keeps the home base stable. Rad is apprenticing into both roles.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived. Shen was away when the attack happened and returned to find the village wounded. Shen felt absent and guilty, though they did nothing wrong. Now Shen is looking for rare medicinal plants that might help the village heal — driven by guilt as much as by responsibility.  
**Current Tensions:** Joy wants Shen home more often. Shen wants to stay in the mountains longer. The attack has made this tension sharper. Rad is old enough to sense it and is choosing sides.  
**Community Path Role:** Shen's knowledge of the wider landscape is valuable for community rebuilding. Also, Shen's wandering-versus-staying tension mirrors the player's own decision about paths.

### FAMILY 10: TORVEN (Militia/Defense)
**Role:** Village defense, creating weapons, training.  
**Members:** Torven (50s, scarred, capable), their partner SABLE (50s, equally skilled), their adult child KESS THE FIGHTER (25, fierce), KESS's partner TORN (25, steady)  
**What They Do:** Torven and Sable are the closest the village has to military expertise. They know how to use weapons, how to organize defensive positions, how to plan for threats. Kess (the fighter, different from Kess the healer) and Torn train with them. Before the attack, this role was almost ceremonial — practice, not real combat. Now it's central.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived the attack by coordinating defense. Torven took injuries but lived. Now Torven is pushing for the village to become *more* martial — better training, better organization, regular drills. Not everyone agrees with this approach.  
**Current Tensions:** Torven believes the attack was a failure of readiness. Others believe it was just bad luck. Torven is fighting to make the village less vulnerable, but this conflicts with values of openness and trust that the village holds. Can a community committed to cooperation still prepare for violence?  
**Community Path Role:** Torven represents security. The community path involves deciding whether security through preparation is worth the cultural cost.

### FAMILY 11: NASH & REESE (Gardeners/Agriculturists)
**Role:** Planning crops, managing soil, seed selection.  
**Members:** Nash (45, slow-thinking but thoughtful), Reese (45, intuitive about plants), their children DRINA (14), SAM (12), and LEAF (10)  
**What They Do:** Nash and Reese manage the village's gardens. They know which crops work in this valley, how to rotate plantings, how to keep the soil healthy. It's meticulous work that no one thinks about until someone starves. Thane (the elder) works with them on seed selection because seeds are precious.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived. The gardens were damaged during the attack — buildings fell into planted areas, trampling crops. Some of the fall's harvest was lost. Nash and Reese are replanting, but winter is coming. They're calculating whether there's enough food.  
**Current Tensions:** Nash and Reese have different views on how aggressive to be with new plantings. Nash wants to be cautious (what if there's another attack?). Reese wants to maximize production (what if there's famine?). The children are growing and need food either way.  
**Community Path Role:** Food security is the foundation of everything. Nash and Reese are essential to the community path.

### FAMILY 12: EMLYN (Trade/Resource Exchange)
**Role:** External relationships, managing what the village trades and exchanges.  
**Members:** Emlyn (50s, smooth-talking, risk-taker), their partner THAL (50s, cautious, detail-oriented), their child IONA (20, curious)  
**What They Do:** Emlyn travels to other settlements, negotiates exchanges, brings back news and goods the village can't produce. Thal manages the inventory. Iona is learning the trade. Before the attack, this was profitable and trusted work. After the attack, Emlyn's willingness to travel has made them suspicious — *where were you when we were attacked?* The trust is fraying.  
**How They Were Affected:** Emlyn was away at the Crossroads when the attack happened. Survived, but returns to accusations and doubt. Some people quietly wonder if Emlyn is a traitor. Emlyn is hurt, defensive, and considering leaving the village.  
**Current Tensions:** Emlyn is essential but untrusted. The village needs external trade, but people are afraid. Can Emlyn rebuild trust? Should Emlyn even try, or is the village just ungrateful?  
**Community Path Role:** Emlyn represents the village's relationship with the wider world. The community path involves deciding whether the village stays isolated or engages with other settlements.

### FAMILY 13: LITHE (Guard/Movement)
**Role:** Keeping watch, checking boundaries, watching for danger.  
**Members:** Lithe (60s, anxious, hypervigilant), their partner DOCK (60s, calm, grounding), their adult child FINN (30, brave), and FINN's two children RIVER (7) and STONE (4, doesn't remember the attack)  
**What They Do:** Lithe essentially invented this role as a response to feeling unsafe. Lithe keeps watch at night, checks boundaries, listens for threats. Before the attack, people thought Lithe was paranoid. After the attack, people think Lithe might have been right. Now Lithe's anxiety has become validated, and Lithe is expanding the watch.  
**How They Were Affected:** Survived. Saw the attack coming and tried to warn people, but wasn't believed fast enough. This confirms every fear Lithe has carried. Lithe is now consumed by trying to prevent another attack.  
**Current Tensions:** Lithe's watchfulness is either protective or oppressive, depending on perspective. Finn wants Lithe to relax, but Lithe is convinced relaxation equals vulnerability. The children are growing up with a parent who is always afraid.  
**Community Path Role:** Lithe represents paranoia and caution. The community path involves deciding whether the village's openness was a flaw or a virtue.

## Village Functions (Distributed Across Families)

### Food System
- **Acquisition:** Kai & Merith (hunters), Lexx (fishing), Shen (foraging)
- **Production:** Nash & Reese (gardening), Thane & Liss (seed management)
- **Preservation:** Renn (preservation techniques), Roan (tool maintenance for food prep)
- **Distribution:** Collective decision each season
- **Vulnerability:** Multiple people know each role, but no one is irreplaceable. The attack proved this could fail.

### Shelter & Infrastructure
- **Construction/Maintenance:** Thorne (DEAD), Jenna (apprentice, now elevated), Roan (tool support)
- **Critical Gap:** With Thorne dead, there's no experienced builder. Jenna is learning as she goes. Structures will slowly decay if no one steps in.
- **Opportunity:** If the player has the aptitude, they could become the village's builder. This is a concrete way to contribute to the community path.

### Health
- **Healing:** Elsa (DEAD), Kess (apprentice, now elevated), Sari (if she stays)
- **Critical Gap:** Medical knowledge is incomplete. Kess is learning from memory. People die from preventable issues.
- **Opportunity:** The mentor researcher's collaboration (if the player pursues knowledge path) could help Kess rebuild Elsa's tradition from documented notes.

### Knowledge & Memory
- **Teaching:** Mira (literacy, history)
- **Skill Transmission:** Elders and experienced people teaching apprentices
- **Documentation:** Mira (journals, records)
- **Vulnerability:** Knowledge is being lost as experienced people carry it without formalizing it.

### External Relations
- **Trade:** Emlyn (trusted/untrusted), Kai & Merith (scouts)
- **Vulnerability:** Emlyn's untrustworthiness is a liability. The village is isolated by choice and by fear.

### Defense
- **Training:** Torven, Sable, Kess the fighter
- **Awareness:** Lithe (watching)
- **Vulnerability:** The village's first defense strategy failed. People are trying different approaches.

## Post-Attack Changes

**Before the Attack:**
- Village was stable, cooperative, self-sufficient
- People trusted each other and external relationships
- Security measures were minimal
- Focus was on thriving, not surviving

**After the Attack:**
- Stability shattered
- Internal trust remains but external trust fractured (Emlyn is suspect)
- Defense measures are being implemented
- Everyone is grieving and trying to figure out if the village can survive

**The Community Path's Central Question:**
Can the village rebuild as what it was (open, cooperative, trusting), or will the trauma force it to become something different (closed, cautious, militarized)?

## Path-Specific Village Evolution

### Community Path
- The player stays. The village gradually heals.
- Families rebuild their roles. Jenna becomes the builder. Kess stabilizes as healer.
- Trust is slowly restored. Emlyn might be forgiven.
- The village thrives (or learns to again).
- The village path becomes a counterweight to the brother's hollowing.

### Wandering Path
- The player leaves. The village continues, but without the player.
- By the time the player returns (late game), the village has changed.
- Some people have left (Emlyn, maybe others).
- Some new people have arrived.
- The village is smaller, different, but still standing.

### Power Path
- The player stays but doesn't choose community.
- The village continues. The player becomes distant from it.
- Village decisions are made without the player's input.
- By late game, the village might respect the player's power or fear it.

### Knowledge Path
- The player stays but focuses on research, not community.
- Kess might collaborate with the player (if the mentor researcher provides guidance).
- The village sees the player as pursuing personal knowledge rather than community good.
- The player's contribution is indirect.
