# The Community NPC — Desperate Anchor

## Character Name and Identity

**Name:** SENNA

(Same name as one of the weaver's children, but this is a different context. Senna is common in post-collapse communities.)

**Full Identity:** SENNA is in their early thirties, a childhood friend of the player. Not the same as Kael (the childhood friend who died in the attack), but someone who shares that history with the player — they grew up together, played together, knew each other before everything changed.

**Role:** Senna is one of the people the village lost someone to. A spouse or a sibling or both. Senna is the face of the village's grief and the vessel of its desperate hope that things can be rebuilt.

**Appearance/Presence:** Senna moves with a particular kind of exhaustion — not the tiredness of a single hard day, but the wearing away of someone who hasn't stopped since the attack. There's something both stronger and more fragile about Senna than before the village was wounded. They're holding together by sheer force of will.

---

## What Senna Lost

**Scenario 1 (Most Emotionally Rich):** Senna lost a spouse in the attack.

- They were together five years. They were planning a commitment ceremony for next spring.
- The spouse died defending others — doing something brave and foolish.
- Senna was elsewhere in the village when it happened, sheltering children. Senna didn't get to say goodbye.
- Now Senna lives in a space that was built for two people. The sleeping platform is still sized for two.

**The Grief:** Not fresh grief — by the time the player meets Senna, weeks have passed. But it's settled into everything. It's not something Senna cries about anymore. It's something Senna *is*.

**Spouse's Name:** KIRA  
(Kira was practical, capable, steady. Kira made decisions quickly. Kira died making a decision to protect people.)

---

## Why Senna Stays

Before the attack, Senna could have left anytime. The village was safe and comfortable, but not so binding that departure was unthinkable. Senna had wanderlust, had thought about seeing other settlements.

After the attack, Senna's options collapsed into a single clarity: *the village needs me, and I cannot leave.*

**Why Senna Stayed (Immediate):**
- Sheltering children during the attack made Senna responsible for their survival.
- Kira's death made Senna part of something larger than personal need.
- Senna became the person who knew where things were, who remembered procedures, who could explain to the children why the attackers came.
- Senna became the person people relied on.

**Why Senna Continues to Stay (Current):**
- The village is still wounded. People still need help.
- Leaving feels like abandoning Kira's death. Like making it meaningless.
- Senna has become necessary. The village notices when Senna is tired or distracted.
- There's a deep, aching need to feel like staying mattered. Like Senna's sacrifice means something.

---

## Senna's Current Role in the Village

Senna is not formally a leader, but informally, Senna is everywhere:

- **With the children:** Senna teaches them, keeps them occupied, explains what's happening in language they can understand.
- **With grieving people:** Senna listens. Senna doesn't offer solutions — just presence.
- **With practical work:** Senna helps with rebuilding, with food preparation, with whatever needs doing.
- **With village decisions:** People ask Senna's opinion. Senna doesn't push, but people listen.

**The Cost:** Senna is doing the work of three people. Senna is running on fumes. Everyone who looks at Senna sees someone who is breaking slowly, trying not to let it show.

---

## Senna's Philosophy

Senna believes deeply in something, and it shows:

*"We lost people. We lost Kira and others. Leaving makes that meaningless. But staying, rebuilding, making this place something worth what we've already paid — that makes it matter. We can't undo the attack. But we can choose whether the village survives it."*

Senna is not naive. Senna knows the village might fail anyway. Knows the world is dangerous. But Senna believes that the only dignified response to loss is *not to lose more by abandoning what was built.*

---

## The Community Pitch (What Senna Asks of the Player)

Senna's first conversation with the player (after the attack, when the player is deciding which path to follow) is the emotional pivot point:

**Senna:** "The brother's leaving. I can't blame him. But... everyone who's capable is leaving. The mentor researcher is at the Crossroads. The traders keep disappearing. Even the brother is going. And if you go too..."

*Senna doesn't finish the sentence. Doesn't need to.*

**Senna:** "I'm not asking you to sacrifice yourself. I'm not asking you to give up magic or power or understanding. I'm just asking you to stay. To help us figure out how to keep going. And maybe... maybe find out that there's something here worth staying for. Not because you have to. Because you want to."

**The Emotional Core:** Senna is not manipulating. Senna is genuinely terrified and genuinely hoping. The pitch is desperate, but it's honest. *"We need you, and I think this needs you too."*

---

## The Flaw: Obligation as Belonging

Senna's greatest strength is also their greatest trap.

Senna is so good at making people feel needed that the player might stay out of *obligation* rather than out of *choice*. Senna asks for help, and the player agrees, and weeks pass, and the player realizes they're not staying for the village — they're staying because Senna would be devastated if they left.

This is the central tension of the community path:

**For the community path to be fulfilled:**
- The player must choose to stay
- Not because Senna begged them to
- But because they've come to love the village and the work
- Because they've built roots that run deeper than obligation

**The Risk:**
- If the player stays *only* for Senna
- If the player resents the village's needs
- If the player feels trapped
- Then the community path becomes a cage, and Senna becomes the warden (unintentionally)

---

## Senna's Vulnerability

Senna knows this, at some level.

Senna is aware that *needing someone so desperately is a terrible basis for love.*

As the game progresses (on the community path), there's a quiet moment where Senna addresses this:

**Senna:** "I needed you to stay. I won't lie about that. But now I need you to *want* to be here. If you're staying out of duty, then I've lost you the same way I lost Kira."

This is the maturation moment. Senna releases the player from obligation and asks for something harder: genuine choice.

## Path-Specific Development

### On the Community Path
- Senna becomes a mentor and a friend.
- The player and Senna work together rebuilding the village.
- Spouse mechanics can develop here (if the player becomes romantically involved with Senna or someone else in the village).
- By late game, Senna is not the desperate anchor anymore — Senna has healed enough to let go.
- Endgame community scene: Senna stands with the player overlooking the rebuilt village, and says something like: "Thank you for staying. For choosing this. For choosing me."

### On Other Paths
- Senna remains the village's emotional core.
- Senna respects the player's choice to leave.
- If the player returns (late game), Senna is different — healed, less desperate, but also marked by the player's absence.
- The final conversation acknowledges the distance: *"I understand why you left. I just wish it hadn't hurt so much."*

---

## The Spouse Mechanics Connection

Senna can be the player's romantic interest on the community path.

**Senna's Hesitation:**
- Senna is grieving Kira.
- Senna is not ready for love right away.
- A romance with the player must develop slowly, built on genuine connection and shared work, not on Senna's desperation.

**If the Romance Happens:**
- It's grounded, real, and hard-won.
- It's about choosing each other *after* establishing that you can survive alone.
- It's a counterweight to the brother's hollowing — choosing to be vulnerable again despite loss.

**If the Romance Doesn't Happen:**
- Senna and the player are still deeply bonded.
- Senna might eventually develop feelings for someone else (another villager, someone new who arrives).
- The community becomes the real love story, not romance.

---

## Senna's Emotional Arc

### Act 1 (Prologue/Early Game)
- Desperate, exhausted, holding on
- Grieving Kira fresh enough that it shapes every interaction
- The pitch to the player is a last act of hope

### Act 2 (Mid-Game)
- If the player stays: Gradually less desperate, more grounded, finding rhythm in rebuilding
- Realizing the player is choosing to stay, which changes everything
- Releasing the grip on obligation

### Act 3 (Late Game)
- Healed enough to be real. Still grieving (always will be), but not broken by it.
- Standing with the player at endgame as a peer, not a suppliant
- Ready to build something new, not just survive what's left

---

## Voice and Dialogue Texture

Senna speaks simply. No flowery language. Senna is too tired for affectation.

**Senna's Speech:**
- Direct when it matters, quiet otherwise
- Uses Kira's name often — not avoiding grief, just acknowledging it
- Asks honest questions: "Are you staying or going? I need to know."
- Doesn't manipulate, but doesn't hide need either

**Example Dialogue:**
- "The village isn't special. But the people are. Kira knew that."
- "I'm not asking you to be Kira. I'm asking you to be you, and that's enough."
- "If you're going to leave, leave now. Don't wait until I've gotten used to having you here."
