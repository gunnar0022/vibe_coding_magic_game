SOUL LEVEL SYSTEM
==================


CORE IDEA
----------

The game does NOT use a traditional experience-based leveling system. Instead, progression is handled through a SOUL LEVEL.


PROGRESSION
------------

- Soul level can ONLY be increased by completing SPECIFIC MILESTONES
- Milestones do NOT always involve combat
- There is no XP gain from enemies
- Soul level increases are curated and finite


STAT EFFECTS
--------------

Each soul level grants SMALL, FLAT INCREASES to:
- MAX HP
- STAMINA
- INNATE MANA

These bonuses are intentionally minor and are NOT the main source of player power.


PRIMARY PURPOSE
----------------

The PRIMARY PURPOSE of soul level is to act as a CHECK for game systems and outcomes.

Soul level may affect:
- Whether certain spell effects (e.g., illusions) work on the player
- Quest availability or branching paths
- Other conditional outcomes throughout the game

Higher soul level does not guarantee better outcomes, only different ones.
