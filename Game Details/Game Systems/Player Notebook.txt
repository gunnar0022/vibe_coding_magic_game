PLAYER NOTEBOOK
================


PURPOSE
--------

The notebook is interactive and optional. It does not directly alter gameplay outcomes. It serves as the player's personal journal for tracking magical knowledge and experimentation.


SYMBOL REFERENCE PAGES
------------------------

Each symbol has a game-defined reference page explaining the symbol as a fundamental unit, without listing combinations.

When new symbols are discovered, minimal information is auto-recorded, primarily contextual:
- A symbol learned from an NPC may come with richer explanation
- A symbol discovered in the wild may initially be recorded as unknown, with only location or circumstance noted


FREEFORM NOTE PAGES
---------------------

Adjacent to each symbol's reference page, the player may create freeform note pages. These pages may contain hypotheses, observations, incorrect conclusions, keywords, or organizational structures defined entirely by the player.

Players may create folders, subfolders, and custom groupings. These can act as personal loadouts, thematic collections, or experimental logs.


DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
------------------

- Players are explicitly allowed to be wrong. Incorrect notes are valid and expected.
- The player will get out of the notebook roughly what they put into it.
- The notebook validates the "memory over UI" premise -- discovery, experimentation, consequence, and learning.
- Once a symbol is recorded, it is never lost. Symbols may be recontextualized as new information is discovered, but prior knowledge is not erased.


SECURITY
---------

The notebook will have to implement input sanitation to prevent any unwanted attacks on the game itself.
