############# SHARED ENVIRONMENTAL MANA SYSTEM #############

CORE PREMISE
Magic is NOT a purely personal resource. Each AREA in the world contains a LARGE, SHARED POOL OF ENVIRONMENTAL MANA, representing the ambient magical energy generated by life in that region.
ALL MAGIC USERS—PLAYER AND ENEMIES—DRAW FROM THIS SAME POOL.

Environmental mana is:

COMPLETELY VISIBLE to the player

AREA-SPECIFIC (each zone has its own pool)

VERY LARGE, but FINITE

SLOWLY REGENERATING over time

At the start of the game, this pool FEELS ABUNDANT, encouraging liberal spell use. Over time, as more magic users enter combat and as encounters grow more complex, players realize the resource is NOT INFINITE and must be managed deliberately.

############# ENVIRONMENTAL PERSISTENCE & REGENERATION #############

Each area tracks its own environmental mana value persistently.

If an area is heavily drained, it will NOT immediately recover.

Leaving and returning later may reveal PARTIAL restoration.

While the player is OUTSIDE an area, only the MANA VALUE is simulated (no full environmental simulation).

Environmental mana regeneration is processed in DISCRETE INTERVALS (e.g., every ~30 seconds) rather than continuously.

Regeneration may be FASTER while the player is absent to prevent excessive backlog.

Magic ALWAYS regenerates eventually. The player is never permanently locked out of progress:

They may WAIT

They may DRAW ON HEALTH

They may use NON-MAGICAL WEAPONS

Certain spaces (e.g., boss arenas) are ISOLATED ENVIRONMENTS that ALWAYS BEGIN FULL to prevent unfair depletion scenarios.

############# MANA TYPES & PRIORITY ORDER #############

The player effectively interacts with MULTIPLE LAYERS of magical energy, consumed in a FIXED PRIORITY ORDER:

ENVIRONMENTAL MANA (shared, primary source)

COLLECTED MANA (temporarily concentrated, personal)

RESERVED MANA (protected personal buffer)

HEALTH (absolute last resort)

ENVIRONMENTAL MANA

The PRIMARY and PRACTICAL source of spellcasting.

Shared among all magic users in the area.

Large but finite.

Slowly regenerates over time.

INNATE / COLLECTED MANA

Represents magic temporarily pulled from the environment and held by the caster.

VERY SMALL by default (often enough for only 1–2 spells).

ALWAYS REGENERATES.

Acts as a short-term buffer, NOT a replacement for environmental mana.

RESERVED MANA

A PERSONAL, PROTECTED pool accessed through specific mechanics and items.

DOES NOT naturally regenerate under normal conditions.

Some advanced spells MAY REQUIRE reserved mana.

Often fueled indirectly through items or by converting health.

HEALTH AS MANA

When all other mana sources are depleted, spells can draw directly from HEALTH.

This is intentional, dangerous, and sometimes necessary.

Sustained magical overuse has consequences, but also enables progression in dead zones.

############# PLAYER LEARNING & DISCOVERY PHILOSOPHY #############

The system is NOT fully explained upfront.

The player initially believes the visible mana bar is “theirs.”

Contradictory evidence emerges naturally:

Enemies casting spells drains the same bar.

Areas revisited later are not fully restored.

NPC dialogue, environmental cues, and repeated exposure build understanding over time.

The goal is NOT immediate mastery, but GRADUAL REALIZATION that the world’s magic operates under deeper rules than first assumed.

############# ITEMS & MANA INTERACTION #############

ALL POTIONS share a COMMON POTION SICKNESS TIMER to prevent stacking abuse.

THE COLLECTOR

Temporarily CONCENTRATES a PORTION of environmental mana into the player.

Does NOT permanently remove mana from the area.

The amount collected is LIMITED by the player’s innate mana capacity.

Enemies can use collectors as well, causing sudden, noticeable drops in environmental mana.

THE STANDARD

Bottled magic stabilized for personal use.

Restores or OVERHEALS RESERVED MANA beyond its normal maximum.

Intended to ALLEVIATE health-drain pressure for a few additional spells, NOT replace environmental mana.

Overhealing is helpful but never mandatory.

THE STIMULANT

A HIGH-RISK, HIGH-REWARD potion.

Temporarily:

Increases ENVIRONMENTAL MANA REGENERATION

Enables SLOW REGENERATION of RESERVED MANA

Followed by a SEVERE DEBUFF period once the effect ends.

Designed as a LAST-RESORT TOOL:

Strong enough to swing a fight

Dangerous if the fight outlasts the buff window

############# DEAD ZONES, REST, AND RECOVERY #############

Some areas may have LITTLE OR NO ENVIRONMENTAL MANA.

In these situations, players must:

Spend HEALTH to cast spells

Rely on RESERVED or COLLECTED mana

Switch to NON-MAGICAL WEAPONS

REST POINTS (camps, homes, safe zones) allow the player to:

Restore their own magic resources

RESET ALL ENVIRONMENTAL MANA POOLS in the world to full

This provides a GLOBAL RESET MECHANISM without trivializing moment-to-moment resource management.

############# DESIGN INTENT SUMMARY #############

This mana system is designed to:

Treat magic as a WORLD RESOURCE, not a personal stat

Encourage adaptation rather than optimization

Force players to engage with multiple systems instead of a single dominant strategy

Create moments where magic feels powerful, scarce, dangerous, and costly—sometimes all at once

The player does not learn the truth of magic through exposition, but through EXPERIENCE, CONTRADICTION, and ADAPTATION.
