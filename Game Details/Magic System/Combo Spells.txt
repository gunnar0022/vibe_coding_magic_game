COMBO SPELLS (TWO SYMBOL COMBINATIONS)
========================================

Order-independent. Entries marked [PROPOSAL] are suggestions following established
patterns -- to be confirmed or changed.


ELEMENTAL + ELEMENTAL
----------------------

火＋水  Steam Cloud         15 mana   5 fire dmg    radius 2    5.0s
  wet 3.0s + obscured. When the player is inflicted with obscure, a dense fog
  covers the screen (white sheet with fog-like opacity for now).

火＋土  Magma Burst         40 mana   15 fire dmg   --          3.0s
  burning 6.0s. Directional cast: cardinal (N/S/E/W) casts produce + shape,
  diagonal casts produce X shape around the caster. Each second re-triggers
  damage. 3 ticks, 15 damage each, reapplying burning to targets in range.

火＋風  Inferno Gust        20 mana   25 fire dmg   radius 2    2.0s
  burning 5.0s. Spreading fire in the wind direction.

火＋生  Null
  No interaction between 火 and 生.

火＋空  Shadow Flame        40 mana   15 dmg        cone        0.3s
  burning + decay. A tight cone of shadowy fire in the direction of the mouse.

火＋光  Solar Burst         40 mana   15 dmg        radius 2    0.5s
  Burns targets and cleanses other status effects. Medium radius around caster.

水＋土  Mud Spray           15 mana   --            radius 3    --
  slowed 5.0s. Creates difficult terrain that halves movement speed.

水＋風  Mist                12 mana   --            radius 3    8.0s
  wet 3.0s. Large obscuring AoE.

水＋生  Rejuvenation        25 mana   -12 (heal)    radius 0    3.0s
  wet 2.0s. Heal happens over 3 ticks, up to 12 health total.

水＋空  Null
  No interaction between 水 and 空.

水＋光  Cleansing Water     50 mana   -5 (heal)     radius 0    0.5s
  Removes negative status effects and heals 5 health.

水＋闇  Null
  No interaction between 水 and 闇.

土＋風  Null
  No interaction between 土 and 風.

土＋生  Summon Boulder      40 mana   --            radius 0    --
  If space is available, a rock object emerges from the ground into the map.
  Behaves like other rocks (collision, pushable, destructible). Snaps to the
  large tile grid, not the sub-grid.

土＋空  Null
  No interaction between 土 and 空.

土＋光  Prismatic Light     30 mana   --            radius 2    4.0s       [PROPOSAL]
  Utility spell. Confuses enemies in range.

風＋生  Null
  No interaction between 風 and 生.

風＋空  Null
  No interaction between 風 and 空.

風＋光  Tailwind            20 mana   --            radius 0    6.0s       [PROPOSAL]
  Hastened status on the caster. Wind and light combine for a speed boost.

生＋空  Null                                                               [PROPOSAL]
  生 (life) and 空 (emptiness) cancel each other out.

生＋光  Purify              20 mana   -5 (heal)     radius 1    1.0s
  Cleanses negative or damaging effects like decay, and heals slightly.

空＋光  Dispel              25 mana   --            radius 2    0.2s       [PROPOSAL]
  Nullifies all active spell effects in the area. Void + light = clean slate.
