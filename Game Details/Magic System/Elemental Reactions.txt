ELEMENTAL REACTIONS
====================

This document defines how elements interact with each other and with object attributes
in the world. These are latent/environmental reactions -- spell combos produce different
(often more dramatic) results than environmental exposure.


ELEMENTS VS ATTRIBUTES
------------------------

ELEMENTS (active forces, can be applied via magic or environment):
  Fire, Water, Ice, Electric, Wind, Earth, Light, Dark, Spirit

ATTRIBUTES (inherent properties of objects, not applied):
  Organic  -- living tissue, can burn, can heal (creatures, player, fabric clothes)
  Plant    -- subset of organic, can grow, rooted (trees, grass, flowers)
  Metal    -- weapons, armor, ore
  Liquid   -- water bodies, potions
  Stone    -- rocks, boulders, walls

Plant and Organic are distinct categories. Plants burn until destroyed when set on
fire; organic things don't necessarily do that. Fabric clothes fall under Organic,
making them burnable. This distinction allows fine-grained reaction rules.


ELEMENT + ELEMENT REACTIONS
-----------------------------

These latent reactions look different than when corresponding spells are combined.
In spells, creations and effects are more likely. Environmental mixing produces
simpler, more subdued results.

FIRE + WATER     = Steam/Evaporation (extinguishes fire, creates steam cloud)
FIRE + ICE       = Melt (extinguishes fire, creates water puddle)
FIRE + WIND      = Spread (fire spreads faster/further)
FIRE + EARTH     = whatever was second remains
FIRE + ELECTRIC  = discharge (brief, high heat effect)
FIRE + LIGHT     = fire overtakes light condition
FIRE + DARK      = fire overtakes dark condition
FIRE + SPIRIT    = fire overtakes spirit condition

WATER + ICE      = Freeze (water becomes ice)
WATER + WIND     = cleanse both statuses
WATER + EARTH    = Mud (slows movement)
WATER + ELECTRIC = Conductivity! (spreads electric damage through water)
WATER + LIGHT    = water overtakes light
WATER + DARK     = overtaken by water
WATER + SPIRIT   = overtaken by water

ICE + WIND       = Blizzard? (living things might take cold damage)
ICE + EARTH      = ice remains
ICE + ELECTRIC   = ice remains
ICE + LIGHT      = ice remains
ICE + DARK       = dark remains
ICE + SPIRIT     = some kind of stun

WIND + EARTH     = cleanse
WIND + ELECTRIC  = nothing
WIND + LIGHT     = light remains
WIND + DARK      = dark remains
WIND + SPIRIT    = spirit remains

EARTH + ELECTRIC = earth remains
EARTH + LIGHT    = earth remains
EARTH + DARK     = dark remains
EARTH + SPIRIT   = spirit remains

ELECTRIC + LIGHT   = full cleanse
ELECTRIC + DARK    = whatever was second remains
ELECTRIC + SPIRIT  = whatever was second remains

LIGHT + DARK     = cleanse
LIGHT + SPIRIT   = spirit remains
DARK + SPIRIT    = fear status


ELEMENT + ATTRIBUTE REACTIONS
-------------------------------

How elements affect objects with specific attributes.

FIRE + ORGANIC   = Burning (damage over time, spreads to adjacent organic)
FIRE + PLANT     = Burns faster than generic organic, spreads fire
FIRE + METAL     = Heating (damages on touch)
FIRE + LIQUID    = Evaporation (liquid disappears, steam created)
FIRE + STONE     = Heating, but dissipates faster than metal

WATER + ORGANIC  = Wet status (extinguishes burning)
WATER + PLANT    = Growth (could hold onto growth status, affect gardens, cause plants to grow)
WATER + METAL    = nothing
WATER + STONE    = Erosion? Slippery surface?

ICE + ORGANIC    = Slow
ICE + PLANT      = Frost damage
ICE + METAL      = nothing
ICE + LIQUID     = Freezes into ice block
ICE + STONE      = nothing

ELECTRIC + ORGANIC = Shock damage, stun
ELECTRIC + PLANT   = nothing special
ELECTRIC + METAL   = Chains to nearby metal
ELECTRIC + LIQUID  = Conducts through entire liquid body
ELECTRIC + STONE   = no effect

WIND + ORGANIC   = Pushed, scattered (seeds spread -- depends on each object)
WIND + PLANT     = similar to organic
WIND + METAL     = no effect
WIND + LIQUID    = no effect
WIND + STONE     = Erosion over long time? Dust?

EARTH + ORGANIC  = nothing
EARTH + PLANT    = nothing
EARTH + METAL    = nothing
EARTH + LIQUID   = make mud
EARTH + STONE    = nothing

LIGHT + ORGANIC  = nothing
LIGHT + PLANT    = nothing
LIGHT + METAL    = nothing
LIGHT + LIQUID   = nothing
LIGHT + STONE    = nothing

DARK + ORGANIC   = Wither
DARK + PLANT     = Wilt, decay
DARK + METAL     = Weaken (swords and armor become less effective)
DARK + LIQUID    = Poison? Tainted
DARK + STONE     = nothing

SPIRIT + ORGANIC = nothing
SPIRIT + PLANT   = nothing
SPIRIT + METAL   = nothing
SPIRIT + LIQUID  = nothing
SPIRIT + STONE   = nothing


CHAIN REACTION RULES
----------------------

Reactions should CASCADE through connected objects.

Example chain (fire spread):
1. Player casts Fire on Tree A
2. Tree A gains Burning status
3. Tree A is adjacent to Tree B (both have Plant attribute)
4. After X seconds, fire spreads to Tree B
5. Tree B was Wet, so fire is extinguished immediately
6. Tree B loses Wet status

Example chain (electric conductivity):
1. Player casts Electric on Water puddle
2. Water has Liquid attribute, Electric + Liquid = Conductivity
3. All entities standing in connected water take shock damage
4. Enemy in water has Metal armor, takes bonus damage

Reaction spread methods (context-dependent, all three should be supported):
- Adjacent tiles only
- Radius-based
- Material-connection based (all connected water)


REACTION PRIORITY
------------------

Use a queue system. If reactions seem simultaneous, use a set priority based on how
likely that element is to overwrite other statuses.


STATUS PERSISTENCE
-------------------

All status effects should have a default timer, but can be overwritten by blending.
Options per effect (all should be possible):
- Timed duration
- Until countered
- Based on intensity/source power


PLAYER ATTRIBUTES
------------------

- Player is Organic by default
- Wearing or holding metal adds temporary Metal tags
- Standing in water applies Wet status, not Liquid attribute
