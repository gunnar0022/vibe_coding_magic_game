SOLO SPELLS (SINGLE SYMBOL CAST)
==================================

Cast by selecting one symbol in the radial menu.


ELEMENTAL SOLO CASTS
---------------------

火 Ember                10 mana   15 fire dmg    radius 0    2.0s    burning 3.0s    destructive
  A small flame is produced in front of the caster.

水 Splash               5 mana    --             radius 0    --      wet 5.0s        environmental
  A puddle of water is produced in front of the caster.
  (wet status applied on leaving the puddle)

土 Null                  10 mana   --             radius 0    --      --              dud
  The 土 symbol does nothing on its own.

風 Gust                  10 mana   --             radius 2    0.5s    --              utility
  A gust effect is created in a cone in the direction the caster is facing.

光 Light                 15 mana   --             radius 2    10.0s   --              utility
  A ring of light is created around the caster.

闇 Darkness              20 mana   -- (dark dot)  radius 0    0.2s    decay 6.0s      status
  A dark bubble appears in front of the caster. Organic creatures are afflicted
  with decay, becoming weaker and taking dark damage until it is lifted.

電 Electric              10 mana   15 electric    radius 2    0.2s    --              destructive
  Electricity sparks in and around the caster, damaging everything in a circular radius.

雷 Thunder               40 mana   50 electric    radius 3    0.3s    electrified     destructive
  A stronger version of 電. Large radius electric burst with temporary electric attribute.

炎 Blaze                 40 mana   50 fire        radius 0    2.0s    burning         destructive
  A stronger version of 火. Intense flames erupt in front of the caster.


FORCE / ABSTRACT SOLO CASTS
-----------------------------

力 Push                  10 mana   10 physical    radius 0    0.1s    --              utility
  Push power 2. Pushes medium rocks and people.

生 Null                  15 mana   --             radius 0    --      --              dud
  The 生 symbol does nothing on its own.

空 Null                  15 mana   --             radius 0    --      --              dud
  The 空 symbol does nothing on its own.

大 Null                  15 mana   --             radius 0    --      --              dud
  The 大 symbol does nothing on its own. It amplifies other symbols in combos.


MODIFIER SOLO CASTS (DUDS)
----------------------------

切 Null                  --        --             --          --      --              dud
  The 切 symbol does nothing on its own. It produces elemental cuts in combos.

石 Null                  --        --             --          --      --              dud
  The 石 symbol does nothing on its own. It enables offensive physical combos.
