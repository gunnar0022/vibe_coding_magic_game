STATUS EFFECTS REFERENCE
=========================


ELEMENTAL STATUS EFFECTS
--------------------------

burning       Damage over time (fire). Spreads to adjacent organic/plant objects.
wet           Vulnerable to electric/ice, extinguishes fire. Applied by water contact.
frozen        Cannot move. Increased physical damage taken.
electrified   Shock damage, chains through water/metal.
chilled       Slowed movement. Precursor to frozen.
heated        Hot to touch. Precursor to burning. Speeds up?


CONDITION STATUS EFFECTS
--------------------------

poisoned      Damage over time.
stunned       Cannot act. Brief immobilization (from electric or other sources).
slowed        Reduced move speed (1.5x cooldown).
hastened      Increased move speed (0.5x cooldown).
muddy         Slowed by mud (1.4x cooldown).
obscured      Vision impaired (fog overlay on screen).


SPECIAL STATUS EFFECTS
------------------------

invisible     Hidden from enemies.
invulnerable  Cannot take damage.
silenced      Cannot cast magic.


DARK / SPIRIT STATUS EFFECTS
------------------------------

feared        From dark + spirit interaction.
cursed        Dark affliction. Resist light?
withered      Dark damage applied to organic targets.
decaying      Dark damage to plants / weakening over time.
weakened      Reduced effectiveness (dark + metal makes equipment less effective).
tainted       Corrupted (dark + liquid interaction).


LIGHT / GROWTH STATUS EFFECTS
-------------------------------

blessed       Light protection, removes dark statuses. Resist dark? (light + spirit)
growing       Plant growth from water contact.


IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
---------------------

- Status effects are tracked via Boolean flags on objects
- When a new effect is applied, interaction checks resolve (e.g., burning + wet = fire extinguished)
- All effects should have a default timer but can be overwritten by blending
- Effects can be timed, until countered, or intensity-based
- The reaction system (src/reactions/) handles element interactions automatically when spells hit
