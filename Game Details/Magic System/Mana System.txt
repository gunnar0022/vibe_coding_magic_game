MANA SYSTEM
============


CORE PREMISE
-------------

Magic is NOT a purely personal resource. Each AREA in the world contains a LARGE, SHARED POOL OF ENVIRONMENTAL MANA, representing the ambient magical energy generated by life in that region. ALL MAGIC USERS -- PLAYER AND ENEMIES -- DRAW FROM THIS SAME POOL.

Environmental mana is:
- COMPLETELY VISIBLE to the player
- AREA-SPECIFIC (each zone has its own pool)
- VERY LARGE, but FINITE
- SLOWLY REGENERATING over time

At the start of the game, this pool FEELS ABUNDANT, encouraging liberal spell use. Over time, as more magic users enter combat and encounters grow more complex, players realize the resource is NOT INFINITE and must be managed deliberately.


MANA TYPES AND PRIORITY ORDER
-------------------------------

The player interacts with MULTIPLE LAYERS of magical energy, consumed in a FIXED PRIORITY ORDER:

1. ENVIRONMENTAL MANA (shared, primary source)
   - The PRIMARY and PRACTICAL source of spellcasting
   - Shared among all magic users in the area
   - Large but finite
   - Slowly regenerates over time

2. COLLECTED MANA (temporarily concentrated, personal)
   - Magic temporarily pulled from the environment and held by the caster
   - VERY SMALL by default (often enough for only 1-2 spells)
   - ALWAYS REGENERATES
   - Acts as a short-term buffer, NOT a replacement for environmental mana

3. RESERVED MANA (protected personal buffer)
   - A PERSONAL, PROTECTED pool accessed through specific mechanics and items
   - DOES NOT naturally regenerate under normal conditions
   - Some advanced spells MAY REQUIRE reserved mana
   - Often fueled indirectly through items or by converting health

4. HEALTH (absolute last resort)
   - When all other mana sources are depleted, spells can draw directly from HEALTH
   - Intentional, dangerous, and sometimes necessary
   - Sustained magical overuse has consequences (see Corruption System)
   - Enables progression in dead zones


ENVIRONMENTAL PERSISTENCE AND REGENERATION
--------------------------------------------

- Each area tracks its own environmental mana value persistently
- If heavily drained, an area will NOT immediately recover
- Leaving and returning later may reveal PARTIAL restoration
- While the player is OUTSIDE an area, only the MANA VALUE is simulated (no full environmental simulation)
- Regeneration is processed in DISCRETE INTERVALS (e.g., every ~30 seconds)
- Regeneration may be FASTER while the player is absent to prevent excessive backlog
- Magic ALWAYS regenerates eventually -- the player is never permanently locked out of progress
- Certain spaces (e.g., boss arenas) are ISOLATED ENVIRONMENTS that ALWAYS BEGIN FULL


MANA ITEMS
-----------

ALL POTIONS share a COMMON POTION SICKNESS TIMER to prevent stacking abuse.

THE COLLECTOR
  - Temporarily CONCENTRATES a PORTION of environmental mana into the player
  - Does NOT permanently remove mana from the area
  - Amount collected is LIMITED by the player's innate mana capacity
  - Enemies can use collectors as well, causing sudden drops in environmental mana

THE STANDARD
  - Bottled magic stabilized for personal use
  - Restores or OVERHEALS RESERVED MANA beyond its normal maximum
  - Intended to ALLEVIATE health-drain pressure for a few additional spells
  - Overhealing is helpful but never mandatory

THE STIMULANT
  - A HIGH-RISK, HIGH-REWARD potion
  - Temporarily: increases environmental mana regeneration and enables slow reserved mana regen
  - Followed by a SEVERE DEBUFF period once the effect ends
  - Designed as a LAST-RESORT TOOL: strong enough to swing a fight, dangerous if the fight outlasts the buff window


DEAD ZONES AND RECOVERY
-------------------------

Some areas may have LITTLE OR NO ENVIRONMENTAL MANA. In these situations, players must:
- Spend HEALTH to cast spells
- Rely on RESERVED or COLLECTED mana
- Switch to NON-MAGICAL WEAPONS

REST POINTS (camps, homes, safe zones) allow the player to:
- Restore their own magic resources
- RESET ALL ENVIRONMENTAL MANA POOLS in the world to full
  (This provides a GLOBAL RESET MECHANISM without trivializing moment-to-moment resource management)


PLAYER LEARNING PHILOSOPHY
----------------------------

The system is NOT fully explained upfront. The player initially believes the visible mana bar is "theirs." Contradictory evidence emerges naturally:
- Enemies casting spells drains the same bar
- Areas revisited later are not fully restored
- NPC dialogue, environmental cues, and repeated exposure build understanding over time

The goal is NOT immediate mastery, but GRADUAL REALIZATION that the world's magic operates under deeper rules than first assumed.
