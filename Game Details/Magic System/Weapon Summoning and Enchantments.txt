MELEE WEAPON ENCHANTMENTS (element + 刀/斧)
---------------------------------------------

Pattern: Enchanted weapons apply their element on hit. Striking drains mana;
if out of mana, the elemental effect does not trigger.

火＋刀  Flame Sword         50 mana   Applies burning on slash. Costs mana per strike.
火＋斧  Flame Axe           55 mana   Same burning enchantment.

水＋刀  Tide Sword          50 mana   Applies wet on slash. Slightly faster swing. Costs mana.
水＋斧  Tide Axe            55 mana   Same water enchantment.

土＋刀  Stone Sword         50 mana   Bludgeoning weapon. Swings slower than 刀, same damage. Costs mana.
土＋斧  Stone Axe           55 mana   Same implementation as 土＋刀.

風＋刀  Gale Blade          50 mana   Minor knockback on slash. Faster swing. Costs mana.    [PROPOSAL]
風＋斧  Gale Axe            55 mana   Same wind enchantment.                                 [PROPOSAL]

力＋刀  Power Blade         50 mana   Push force 1 on hit. Costs mana.                       [PROPOSAL]
力＋斧  Power Axe           55 mana   Push force 1 on hit. Costs mana.                       [PROPOSAL]

生＋刀  Null                No meaningful life enchant for weapons.                           [PROPOSAL]
生＋斧  Null                No meaningful life enchant for weapons.                           [PROPOSAL]

空＋刀  Void Blade          55 mana   Drains HP on hit. Costs mana.                          [PROPOSAL]
空＋斧  Death Axe           60 mana   Drains HP on hit. Costs mana.

光＋刀  Radiant Blade       50 mana   Cleanses caster's negative status on hit. Costs mana.  [PROPOSAL]
光＋斧  Radiant Axe         55 mana   Same cleanse enchantment.                              [PROPOSAL]


BOW ENCHANTMENTS (element + 弓)
---------------------------------

Pattern: Enchanted bows modify arrow properties. Generally cost more mana per
arrow compared to base 弓, with element-specific tradeoffs.

火＋弓  Fire Bow            70 mana   +3 mp/arrow, 1.2s draw. Arrows apply burning on hit.
水＋弓  Water Bow           70 mana   +3 mp/arrow, less physical damage (15). Arrows apply wet.
土＋弓  Stone Bow           70 mana   +5 mp/arrow, 1.4s draw, 9 spd, 30 bludgeoning damage.
風＋弓  Wind Bow            65 mana   Same mp/arrow, 16 spd, range 20, 15 damage.            [PROPOSAL]
力＋弓  Power Bow           70 mana   +5 mp/arrow, 1.3s draw, 30 damage, knockback.          [PROPOSAL]
生＋弓  Null                No meaningful life enchant for bows.                              [PROPOSAL]
空＋弓  Void Bow            75 mana   +5 mp/arrow, 1.4s draw, 15 damage. Afflicts decay.
光＋弓  Light Bow           70 mana   +2 mp/arrow, 15 spd. Cleanses one negative status.     [PROPOSAL]


PROJECTILE SPELLS
------------------

Spells that use the projectile system (like 弓 arrows but magic-based).
Fire toward the mouse cursor at arbitrary angles.

Currently projectile-based:
- 弓 arrows (and all bow enchantments)

Candidates for future projectile conversion:
- 土＋力 Boulder Throw -- could be a slow, heavy projectile
- 水＋力 Water Jet -- could be a fast water bolt
- 雷＋力 (future) -- lightning bolt projectile
- 切 combos -- could produce slashing projectile arcs

Projectile properties to define per spell:
- Speed (tiles/sec)           - Damage + type
- Hitbox size (sub-tile)      - Max range (tiles)
- Status effects on hit       - Piercing vs stopping on hit
- AoE on impact?              - Mana cost / charge time
