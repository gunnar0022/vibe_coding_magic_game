CORRUPTION SYSTEM — THE HOLLOWING
===================================


CORE PRINCIPLE
---------------

Magic itself is not the poison. Using magic from a full environment is safe. The world gives freely when it has something to give.

Corruption begins when you take from yourself. When the environmental mana pool is drained and you continue casting, you draw from your own health — your own being. Each time you do this, it carves a gap in your soul. That gap is filled with nothing. Emptiness.

That emptiness gets filled by whatever is loudest inside you. Grief, pride, hunger, fear, wrath, ambition — whatever your dominant drives are rush in to fill the hollow space. This is the amplification. Magic doesn't inject corruption. It creates the void. Your own nature fills it.


HEALTH-TO-MANA CONVERSION
---------------------------

When environmental, collected, and reserved mana are depleted, the player may CAST SPELLS USING HEALTH.

Key principles:
- Health-to-mana conversion is ALWAYS AVAILABLE
- Conversion is CONSISTENT and PREDICTABLE (no RNG, no misfires)
- It is designed to feel CONVENIENT, not desperate
- In most situations, the player will not hesitate to trade some health for additional spells

This convenience is what makes it dangerous. The cost accumulates invisibly. The mana bar is not just a resource tracker — it is the world telling you to stop. To wait. To let it breathe. The player who respects that boundary is safe. The player who pushes past it is choosing to hollow themselves out.

The game does not punish you for using magic. It punishes you for refusing to stop.


THE HOLLOWING
--------------

The process is gradual. Invisible. You don't feel it happening. You just become MORE of what you already were, and less of everything else.

Each health-to-mana conversion updates HIDDEN TRACKERS recording:
- TOTAL AMOUNT of health converted into mana (cumulative)

These trackers are NOT visible to the player. At specific, predefined THRESHOLDS, the hollowing manifests through:
- Dialogue changes (NPCs react to the player differently)
- Narrative events
- Event branch divergence
- The brother's reactions shift based on the player's state

Early thresholds may feel BENEFICIAL or ambiguous, encouraging continued use.


THE CORRUPTION SPECTRUM
------------------------

The complexity of the transformation mirrors the complexity of the motive that fills the void.

SIMPLE CORRUPTION
  A desperate, hungry group that hollowed themselves out trying to survive becomes
  something simple — feral humanoid creatures with one drive, one purpose, stripped
  of everything else. Bestial. Easy to recognize. Openly hostile. These are the
  remnants of simple desperation.

COMPLEX CORRUPTION
  A person carrying grief, pride, protectiveness, wrath, and genuine love — if they
  hollow enough of themselves out, those drives expand to fill the space but retain
  their complexity. The result can still think. Still plan. Still feel twisted
  versions of what it felt before. Not a monster — a tragedy with teeth. The most
  dangerous corrupted beings in this world are the ones that can still talk to you.

The seven deadly sins work as a DESIGN LENS, not a literal in-game system. The dominant drive determines the flavor of transformation. Hunger becomes gluttony. Fear becomes something that hoards and hides. Ambition becomes something that can never stop acquiring. Pride becomes something that must dominate. More complex characters, holding multiple drives simultaneously, produce more complex and more terrifying results.

Every monster the player fights was once a person who emptied themselves out. The simple ones were driven by simple needs. The complex ones had complex hearts.


THE WORLD IS REGENERATIVE
---------------------------

There is no "spreading darkness" across the map. Drained areas recover as life breathes mana back. Corruption zones are temporary. The land always heals.

The danger is entirely BEHAVIORAL. Settlements don't fall because corruption spread to them — they fall because people made choices under pressure and refused to stop taking. The land under them recovered after they were gone.

The world is fundamentally generous. The tragedy is always human.


CORRUPTION PROGRESSION
------------------------

EARLY HOLLOWING
  - No explicit feedback to the player
  - Subtle narrative hints only
  - Mechanical advantages may outweigh visible drawbacks

MID HOLLOWING
  - NPCs reference historical or contemporary cases of those who "went too far"
  - Observable changes in how the world responds to the player
  - Dialogue options shift in tone

LATE HOLLOWING
  - Identity-altering consequences
  - Access to stronger, more dangerous magic
  - The gap between the player's self-image and their actual state is wide
  - The world reacts with fear, hostility, or resignation

Corruption is a deliberate slide, not a sudden reveal. By mid-game, an active-path player should understand enough about the hollowing to make any further steps a deliberate, informed choice.


PATH-DEPENDENT CORRUPTION RISK
---------------------------------

Active paths (Strength/Power, Knowledge/Understanding):
  - Push hardest, drain most, rest least
  - The Strength path carries the HIGHEST corruption risk
  - Knowledge path carries MODERATE risk — the mentor's caution provides a natural check

Passive paths (Community/Rootedness, Wandering/Detachment):
  - VERY LOW corruption risk
  - Not because pacifism is rewarded, but because the lifestyle doesn't create the void
  - Community: surrounded by living village generating ambient mana
  - Wandering: moving through untouched landscapes, never draining any one place


DESIGN CONSTRAINTS
-------------------

- The game NEVER permanently deceives the player without providing subtle tells
- Corruption is supported by lore, dialogue, and prior evidence
- Players are not punished for acting on information the game presented as reliable
- Corruption should feel like a deliberate slide, not an accidental failure state


OPEN PARAMETERS (INTENTIONALLY UNDEFINED)
-------------------------------------------

The following are deliberately left for later design passes:
- Exact corruption thresholds
- Numerical health-to-mana conversion rates
- Specific spell unlocks tied to corruption
- Full corruption reversal limits (if any)
