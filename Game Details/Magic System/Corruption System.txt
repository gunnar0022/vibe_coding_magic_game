CORRUPTION SYSTEM
==================


CORE DESIGN GOAL
-----------------

Magic must be INHERENTLY DANGEROUS, not through randomness or immediate punishment, but through LONG-TERM, ACCUMULATED CONSEQUENCES that emerge naturally from player decisions.

The player is never told outright that magic is corrupting them. Instead, they gradually discover that USING MAGIC BEYOND SAFE LIMITS changes both their body and their perception of reality.


HEALTH-TO-MANA CONVERSION
---------------------------

When environmental, collected, and reserved mana are depleted, the player may CAST SPELLS USING HEALTH.

Key principles:
- Health-to-mana conversion is ALWAYS AVAILABLE
- Conversion is CONSISTENT and PREDICTABLE (no RNG, no misfires)
- It is designed to feel CONVENIENT, not desperate
- In most situations, the player will not hesitate to trade some health for additional spells
- This ensures the mechanic is USED FREQUENTLY and NATURALLY rather than avoided


HIDDEN CORRUPTION TRACKERS
----------------------------

Each time the player casts spells using HEALTH, the game updates HIDDEN TRACKERS that record:
- TOTAL AMOUNT of health converted into mana
- TYPE OF SPELL CAST (destruction, utility, summon, etc.)

These trackers are NOT visible to the player initially.

At specific, predefined THRESHOLDS, corruption manifests through:
- New spell unlocks
- Narrative events
- Dialogue changes
- Perceptual anomalies
- Event branch divergence

Early thresholds are mostly BENEFICIAL or ambiguous, encouraging continued use.


CORRUPTION PROGRESSION
------------------------

Corruption is NOT A SINGLE METER, but a GRADUAL ARC.

EARLY CORRUPTION
  - No explicit feedback
  - Subtle narrative hints only
  - Mechanical advantages outweigh drawbacks

MID CORRUPTION
  - Noticeable perceptual irregularities
  - NPCs reference historical or contemporary cases of magic abuse
  - Player gains access to RELIABLE METHODS to MANAGE or REDUCE corruption

LATE CORRUPTION
  - Identity-altering consequences
  - Stronger, more dangerous magic access
  - Fewer reversal options
  - The world reacts differently to the player

Corruption is NOT intended to be an accidental permanent failure state.


PERCEPTUAL ANOMALIES (HALLUCINATIONS)
---------------------------------------

Hallucinations are a CONTROLLED, NARRATIVE-DRIVEN mechanic.

They are:
- RARE
- CONTEXTUAL
- NEVER RANDOM

INTRODUCTORY ANOMALIES
  - Occur 1-2 times in LOW-STAKES encounters
  - Example: hostile creature later revealed to have been an animal
  - No mechanical punishment
  - Purpose is to introduce DOUBT in perception

These events establish that the player's senses may be unreliable under corruption, without breaking trust.


MAJOR CORRUPTION QUEST
------------------------

A SINGLE, STORY-CRITICAL QUEST fully explores magical corruption.

Structure:
1. Lore is introduced about others who have "gone too far" with magic
2. During the climax, the player perceives ALLIES as monstrous, hostile entities
3. Internal dialogue supports the hallucinated interpretation
4. Brief, intermittent visual flashes reveal reality: allies are normal, the player is corrupted
5. The player is never explicitly told which perception is correct

RESOLUTION PATHS:

OPTION 1: ACCEPT DEFEAT
  - The player allows their allies to "kill" them
  - The hallucination ends
  - Corruption is SIGNIFICANTLY REDUCED
  - The player survives
  - A RELIABLE corruption-management method is unlocked
  - Long-term narrative consequences reflect restraint and humility

OPTION 2: KILL THE ALLIES
  - The player commits to their perceived reality
  - Allies die permanently
  - Corruption deepens or stabilizes at a high level
  - The world reacts accordingly in future events and dialogue

Both outcomes are VALID and have LASTING CONSEQUENCES.


DESIGN CONSTRAINTS
-------------------

- Hallucinations are NEVER free-roaming or systemic
- The game NEVER permanently deceives the player without providing subtle tells
- Perceptual distortion is supported by lore, dialogue, and prior evidence
- Players are not punished for acting on information the game presented as reliable


OPEN PARAMETERS (INTENTIONALLY UNDEFINED)
-------------------------------------------

The following are deliberately left for later design passes:
- Exact corruption thresholds
- Numerical health-to-mana conversion rates
- Specific spell unlocks tied to corruption
- Full corruption reversal limits
