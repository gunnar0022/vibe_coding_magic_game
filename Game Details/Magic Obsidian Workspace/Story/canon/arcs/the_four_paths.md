# The Four Compass Paths

## The Shared Emotional Question

"What do you do with loss?"

- **Power** says: "Prevent it."
- **Knowledge** says: "Understand it."
- **Community** says: "Rebuild from it."
- **Wandering** says: "Accept it."

## Path-Specific Tradeoffs

### Knowledge / Researcher
- Keeps the brother. Understands the world through hardship.
- Richest NPC relationships (researcher + mentor + brother).
- May have missed parts of the world. Larger community of contacts.

### Strength / Power
- Keeps the brother. Becomes strong.
- Costs are severe — hollowing, damage done, relationships strained.
- Most power and most risk.

### Community / Farmer
- Gains deep roots. Village thrives. Potential spouse. Real belonging.
- Loses the brother in the harshest way — he spirals alone because you stayed.

### Wandering / Detachment
- Sees the most. Experiences the most beauty.
- Loses the brother and the community left behind. Beautiful loneliness.

## Design Principle

None should leave a bad taste. Each is a valid answer to loss, with real gains and real costs.

## Mixed-Path Players

A player who switches emphasis mid-game doesn't break the story — they shift their angle on the same unfolding reality. The player who samples multiple paths gets a broader but shallower picture. The player who commits deeply gets a narrower but richer one. Both are valid.
