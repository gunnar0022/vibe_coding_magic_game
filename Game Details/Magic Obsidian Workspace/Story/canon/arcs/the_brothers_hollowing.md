# The Brother's Hollowing Arc

## Overview

The brother's corruption is the emotional constant of the game. No matter what path the player takes, his arc is happening. The power player walks beside him. The researcher encounters him at the same sites. The farmer hears about him from travelers. The wanderer finds traces of his passage.

## Locked Progression

### Before the Attack
- Reliable, warm, excited about life, proud of praise from parents.

### After the Attack
- Grief channeled into intensity. Determination. Stubbornness. Duty he can't articulate.
- Can't stay in the village. Must go. Leaves.

### Act 1
- Already gone. NPCs mention him. He passed through a neighboring settlement. Was intense. Asked questions no one could answer. Left quickly.

### Act 2 (Active Paths)
- Seems fine at first. Intense, driven, but not obviously falling.
- The change is gradual — visible only when comparing now to six months ago.
- A little harder each reunion. A little less willing to rest. A little more certain that force is the only answer.

### Act 2 (Passive Paths)
- Moves on alone. His hollowing advances without the player's influence.
- The player hears about him secondhand (community) or finds traces (wandering).

### Act 3 — Four Outcomes
1. **Community ending:** Spiraled alone. Became a complex corrupted threat.
2. **Wandering ending:** Consumed or vanished. Fate may be unknown.
3. **Consolation (Knowledge):** At the edge. Player's presence was the counterweight. Recovery begins.
4. **Corruption (Power):** Both hollowed. Attack the mentor together. Become what attacked their village.

## The Mechanism

- He pushes into drained areas and keeps casting. Doesn't rest. Doesn't wait.
- Takes from himself because waiting feels like surrender.
- Acts noble the entire time. The magic doesn't care about justifications.
- The gap between self-image and actual state grows invisibly.

## Key Design Principle

His fate is determined by the cumulative weight of every fork the player chose across Acts 1 and 2. How many times did you walk beside him? What was your compass state? How many times did you leave him alone?
