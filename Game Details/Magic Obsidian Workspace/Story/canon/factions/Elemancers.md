### Elemancy

A community with a narrow discovery experience. They found kanji that produce elemental effects — fire, water, earth, wind — and concluded that element manipulation is what magic *is*. Their framework is confident, internally consistent, and fundamentally incomplete.

Elemancers might genuinely not believe non-elemental magic exists. Show them a healing spell and they'll try to fit it into their model — "that's water-element purification" or "that's earth-element body reinforcement." Show them something that truly can't be categorized elementally and they'll either reject it as fake or experience a crisis of understanding. Their framework is a box they've built around their own experience, and they mistake the box for the world.

The strength of this community is focus. Because they believe only elemental magic is real, they've poured all their experimentation into elemental kanji. They may have discovered pairings and techniques within the elemental domain that broader-minded communities missed simply because nobody else was looking that hard in that specific direction. Narrow belief, deep expertise.

The weakness is rigidity. When reality exceeds their model — and it will — they either adapt or break. An Elemancy community encountering the hollowing for the first time has no framework for it. Elements don't corrupt people. Fire doesn't hollow your soul. Their entire explanatory structure fails at the moment they need it most.
