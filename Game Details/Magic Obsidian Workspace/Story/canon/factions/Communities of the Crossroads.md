This is the 4 communities around the main crossroads. 

Not necessarily unified, but they know they're all neighbors and have slowly grown a level of trust. 

They are aware of each other, but there's no infrastructure between them and they tend not to visit each other villages, only meet at the crossroads. 

They're not aware of the big world around them. 

Importantly, this community consists of none of the other communities with specific names. 

Just North, South, East, and West villages with now significant establishments in the world. They know each other, and that's it.
