# The Player's Parents


## Known Relationships

- **The Player** — their child. The loss defines the game's emotional foundation.
- **The Brother** — their older child. His pride came from their approval. Their death transforms his personality entirely.


---

## Names & Roles

**Thorne** (the father) — Village carpenter and builder.
**Elsa** (the mother) — Midwife, herbalist, and keeper of stores.

## Character Texture

**Thorne** is the kind of quiet that comes from confidence in skill, not from shyness. He doesn't talk much.

The brother loves him and works to make him proud.

**Elsa** is warm.

Both parents valued *contribution*.
