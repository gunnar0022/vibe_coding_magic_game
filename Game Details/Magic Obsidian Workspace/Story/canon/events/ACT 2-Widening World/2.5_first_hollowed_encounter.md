# First Hollowed Encounter

## Placement
- Mid Act 2. After the player has some research context. They encounter a hollowed creature with enough understanding to start connecting dots.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- A hollowed creature (feral, simple — similar to the village attackers but encountered with new context)
- Possibly Venn or Sael

## What Happens
- The player encounters a hollowed creature in the field — not the village attackers, but something similar. Feral, driven by a single impulse, visibly not-quite-human.
- Unlike the village attack (which was pure survival terror), the player now has context. They've been studying with Eloth. They can start to see the hollowing for what it is — not a random monster, but a person who emptied themselves out.
- The encounter may involve combat, observation, or both. The player has the option to study the creature, not just fight it.
- If Venn is present, she's already analyzing. If Sael is present, he's already fighting.

## Key Beats
- The player's reaction should be shaped by accumulated knowledge. This isn't just a threat — it's evidence.
- The connection between this creature and the village attackers should start forming. Same phenomenon, same mechanism.
- This is the first time the player sees hollowing outside the context of "monsters attacked my home." It becomes a pattern, not an incident.
