# First Reunion with the Brother

## Placement
- Early-to-mid Act 2. After the player has begun settling into the Crossroads/research life. Enough time has passed for Sael to have been on his own, pushing hard.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Sael

## What Happens
- The player encounters Sael again for the first time since his departure.
- He's been busy. He's stronger. He's been fighting things, testing himself, pushing into dangerous territory.
- He's genuinely happy to see the player — the warmth is real. But something is different. He's more intense. More focused. Less willing to sit still.
- He might ask the player to come with him on something, or share what he's found. His energy is "we need to be stronger" where Venn's was "do you realize what this means?"
- The player now has context from the research side to start noticing things about Sael's behavior — how hard he's pushing, how little he rests.

## Key Beats
- The reunion should be emotionally warm but subtly wrong. The player is glad to see him. Something feels off.
- Sael is not hostile or antagonistic. He's the same person — just more so. The hollowing hasn't changed his personality, it's amplifying it.
- This is the first time the player holds two frameworks simultaneously: Sael's intensity and Eloth's caution.
