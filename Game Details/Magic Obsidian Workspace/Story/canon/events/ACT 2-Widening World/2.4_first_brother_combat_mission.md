# First Brother Combat Mission

## Placement
- Mid Act 2. After the first reunion. Sael wants the player alongside him for something dangerous.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Sael

## What Happens
- Sael brings the player along on a combat-oriented mission — clearing a threat, investigating something dangerous, or pushing into territory others avoid.
- The player sees Sael's approach to magic firsthand: aggressive, relentless, effective. He doesn't stop when he should. He draws from depleted areas without hesitation.
- The contrast with research missions is stark. With Venn, the player observes and records. With Sael, the player fights and survives.
- Sael is impressive. He's genuinely strong. The player might admire what he's become — or be alarmed by it.

## Key Beats
- The player experiences the seductive side of power. Sael's approach *works*. He wins fights. He survives things others wouldn't.
- The cost is invisible in the moment. The mana bar depletes, health-to-mana conversion kicks in, and it feels convenient. That's the trap.
- This mission is the brother's thesis statement: strength protects the people you love. The question is what it costs.
