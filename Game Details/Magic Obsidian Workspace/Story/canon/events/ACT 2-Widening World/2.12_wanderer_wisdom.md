# Wanderer Wisdom

## Placement
- Mid-to-late Act 2. The player encounters Rahn again — a deeper conversation than the first meeting.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Rahn

## What Happens
- The player finds Rahn at another destination. He's already there, as always.
- This time, the conversation goes deeper. Rahn shares more about his perspective — the pattern he's seen in communities, the way people destroy themselves through refusal to accept limitation.
- He doesn't lecture. He tells stories. Settlements that overfished. Communities that burned every tree. People who worked themselves to death because stopping felt like surrender.
- He may mention Ven obliquely — someone he lost. Not the full story, but enough to hint that his detachment was purchased at a real cost.
- The player is further along in their journey now. Rahn's observations land differently with context.

## Key Beats
- Rahn's wisdom is genuine but incomplete. He's right about the pattern. He's absent for the fight.
- The parallel between his stories and Sael's trajectory should be visible to the player without anyone drawing it explicitly.
- Rahn doesn't tell the player what to do. He shares what he's seen. The player draws their own conclusions.
- This is the mid-game Rahn beat — deeper than the first meeting, not yet the final warning of Act 3.
