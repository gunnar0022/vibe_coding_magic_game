# Finding Natural Kanji

## Placement
- Mid Act 2. During exploration or a research mission. A discovery moment.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Possibly Venn or Eloth

## What Happens
- The player discovers kanji occurring naturally — symbols that formed without human intervention, shaped by the environment or by mana flows organizing themselves over time.
- This challenges the assumption that kanji are a human tool. The symbols exist independently of human use. Humans didn't invent them — they found them.
- The discovery has implications for understanding what magic actually is. If kanji form naturally, the relationship between mana and symbols is deeper than anyone realized.

## Key Beats
- A genuine "what does this mean?" moment. Not a gameplay unlock — a conceptual shift.
- If Venn is present, she's electrified. If Eloth hears about it, he's cautiously fascinated.
- This feeds into the collaborative body of work and may connect to the Act 3 breakthrough.
- Raises the question: what else about magic exists that humans haven't discovered yet?
