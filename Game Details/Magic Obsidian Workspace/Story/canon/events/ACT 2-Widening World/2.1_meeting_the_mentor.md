# Meeting the Mentor

## Placement
- Early Act 2. Venn brings the player to meet Eloth at his research camp. This is the player's entry into the research partnership.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Eloth
- Venn

## What Happens
- Venn takes the player to Eloth's research camp — semi-permanent, in a safe stretch of forest.
- Eloth sizes up the player. He's direct, not warm. Offers genuine partnership under specific conditions: test everything, ask before experimenting, document everything.
- The player gets their first exposure to a rigorous, systematic approach to magic — contrasting with every superstition and fear-based framework they've encountered.
- Eloth's caution is immediately visible. He holds back certain findings. He's not secretive out of selfishness — he's seen what happens when people get tools before wisdom.

## Key Beats
- Eloth should feel grounded and slightly intimidating. He sees the player clearly and is evaluating whether they're worth the risk.
- The three-way dynamic begins: Eloth's caution, Venn's enthusiasm, the player between them.
- The collaborative body of work starts here. Every research mission from this point contributes to it.
