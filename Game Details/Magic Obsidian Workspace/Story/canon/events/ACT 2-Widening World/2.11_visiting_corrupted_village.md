# Visiting a Corrupted Village

## Placement
- Mid-to-late Act 2. After the player has enough understanding of hollowing to read the signs.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Villagers in various states of early corruption
- Possibly a companion

## What Happens
- The player visits a settlement that is actively in the process of failing. Not yet fully hollowed — people are still people — but the signs are there.
- Overuse of magic, depleted local mana, residents who are starting to change. Some are aware something is wrong. Some are in denial. Some think they're fine.
- The player can see the trajectory because they've studied it with Eloth. These people can't — they're inside it.
- The player may be able to warn them, help them, or simply observe. Whether the village listens is another question.

## Key Beats
- This is hollowing in process, not hollowing complete. The horror is that it's still happening. These are still people. They're just losing pieces of themselves.
- The player's knowledge creates a painful gap — they understand what's coming, the villagers don't.
- The village's superstitions and coping mechanisms are on display. Some are accidentally protective. Some are accelerating the problem.
- Connects to the broader theme: communities fail because of choices, not because darkness spreads.
