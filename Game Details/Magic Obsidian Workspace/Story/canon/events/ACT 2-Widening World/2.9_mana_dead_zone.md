# Mana Dead Zone

## Placement
- Mid Act 2. During exploration. The player enters an area where mana has been completely drained.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Possibly a companion

## What Happens
- The player enters a region where the ambient mana pool is empty. The air feels different — flat, dead, wrong. Plants are stunted. Wildlife is absent or skittish.
- Magic doesn't work here, or works weakly. The player's casting is impaired. The safety net is gone.
- The cause could be natural (a mass death site that hasn't recovered), or evidence of someone draining the area through overcasting.
- The experience is visceral — the player has been taking mana for granted. Now it's gone, and they feel the absence.

## Key Beats
- The dead zone teaches through experience what Eloth teaches through words: the pool is finite. The world can be emptied.
- The vulnerability of being unable to cast forces the player to confront their dependence on magic.
- If the dead zone was caused by overcasting, it's a preview of what widespread hollowing does to the land.
- The land will recover — life breathes mana back. But recovery takes time. The world is generous, not infinite.
