# Crossroads Tension

## Placement
- Mid Act 2. After the player has been operating out of the Crossroads for a while. Tensions within the community surface.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Crossroads residents
- Possibly factions or groups with competing interests

## What Happens
- Tensions within the Crossroads come to a head — competing approaches to magic, resource disputes, disagreements about how to handle threats, or friction between established residents and newcomers.
- The Crossroads isn't a unified community. It's a hub where different groups coexist uneasily. The longer the player is there, the more the fault lines become visible.
- The player may be asked to mediate, take sides, or simply witness the friction.

## Key Beats
- The Crossroads should feel like a real community with real problems, not just a quest hub.
- The tensions should connect to the game's themes — how communities handle fear, scarcity, and the unknown.
- The player's actions here can affect their standing with different groups and shape the Crossroads' trajectory.
