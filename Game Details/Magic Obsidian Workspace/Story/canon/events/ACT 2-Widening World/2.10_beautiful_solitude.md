# Beautiful Solitude

## Placement
- Mid Act 2. A quieter moment between heavier narrative beats. The player experiences the world alone.

## Who's Involved
- The player (alone)

## What Happens
- A contemplative moment. The player is alone in the world — no companions, no mission, no urgency. Just the landscape.
- The world is beautiful. The post-Fall environment, saturated with mana over decades, has become something extraordinary. The player has space to notice.
- This is a breathing room event — no combat, no dialogue, no choices. Just presence in a world that's worth experiencing for its own sake.
- Could be tied to a specific location (a ridge, a clearing, a river), or triggered by the player simply being alone in the wild long enough.

## Key Beats
- The wandering life has a real appeal. This moment shows why Rahn walks.
- Beauty isn't a reward — it's the default state of a world that isn't being drained.
- Contrast with the mana dead zone: this is what the world looks like when it's left alone.
- A moment of peace that the player earns by being present, not by completing a task.
