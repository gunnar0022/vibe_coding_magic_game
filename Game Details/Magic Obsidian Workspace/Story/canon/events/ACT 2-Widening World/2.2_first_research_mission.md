# First Research Mission

## Placement
- Early Act 2. After meeting Eloth. The first collaborative field assignment.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Venn (field companion)
- Eloth (directs from camp, reviews findings)

## What Happens
- Eloth sends the player and Venn on a research task — collecting data, observing phenomena, testing a hypothesis in the field.
- The mission introduces the research gameplay loop: go out, observe, record, return, discuss findings with Eloth.
- Venn is an enthusiastic field partner. She talks fast, makes connections, gets excited about details. She also takes risks Eloth wouldn't approve of.
- The player contributes observations from their notebook. The data they bring back matters.

## Key Beats
- The research should feel like genuine discovery, not fetch quests. The player is contributing to understanding.
- Venn's energy and Eloth's caution create productive tension even in absentia — Venn pushes past boundaries Eloth set.
- The findings from this mission add to the collaborative body of work that pays off in Act 3's breakthrough.
