# The Fall (The Collapse)

## Locked Endpoints

- Approximately 50 years before the game begins, civilization collapsed. Instantaneous, catastrophic, irreversible.
- All modern technology rendered inert. Devices failed instantly and permanently.
- Infrastructure collapsed overnight. Production, communication, transportation, medicine — all ceased.
- Many people died. Others fled urban centers for wild regions.
- This was NOT a slow decline. It was an abrupt severing.
- The Fall is NOT the discovery of magic. These are separate events separated by roughly 24 years.

## Consequences

- Plant life reclaimed structures at unnatural pace. Ruins consumed within decades.
- Mass death at concentrated sites created conditions for ambient mana to pool and organize. This process occurred passively over decades — no human casting involved.
- Survivors formed small cooperative communities. Trust became local. Strangers were not welcomed easily.
- The mentality of the collapse: community must be preserved at all costs.


## The Discovery of Magic (~1 Year Ago)

- Mana has always been ambient in the world — a field generated by living systems, cycling through the environment like breath.
- Approximately one year before the game begins, people across the world began discovering, almost simultaneously, that they could channel mana through kanji symbols.
- No one knows why this became possible when it did. The ability appeared suddenly and globally.
- Early magic users were unskilled and unprepared. Many hollowed without understanding the cost.
- Diverging interpretations of magic contributed to fresh instability — communities that had been stable for years suddenly faced new dangers.
- Magical knowledge is raw, new, being invented in real time. No deep traditions exist. No accumulated folk wisdom. Only fresh, panicked interpretations.

## Timeline

- The collapse occurred ~50-60 years before the game. Elders remember it vaguely.
- Magic was discovered ~1 year before the game. Everyone is still figuring it out.
- The 50-60-year gap matters: communities had time to stabilize after the collapse before magic destabilized them again.
