# Receive the Notebook

## Placement
- After the attack aftermath. The player recovers or is given Kael's notebook/spell book.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Kael (through what he left behind)

## What Happens
- The player comes into possession of the notebook that Kael kept — his observations, his notes on the magic field, whatever he was working on.
- This becomes the player's notebook going forward — a core game system for recording discoveries, spells, and research.
- The notebook carries emotional weight. It belonged to someone who saw the world with unusual sensitivity. Now the player carries that perspective forward.

## Key Beats
- The notebook is Kael's legacy — his way of seeing continues through the player.
- Mechanically, this introduces the player notebook system.
- The early entries should be in Kael's voice/handwriting — a ghost in the pages.
