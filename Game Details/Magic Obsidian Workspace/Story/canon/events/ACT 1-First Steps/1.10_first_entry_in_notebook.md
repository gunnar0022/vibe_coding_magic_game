# First Entry in the Notebook

## Placement
- Shortly after receiving the notebook. The player makes their own first entry.

## Who's Involved
- The player

## What Happens
- The player writes their first entry in Kael's notebook — making it *their* notebook.
- This could be prompted by a discovery, a thought, or simply the need to record something after everything that's happened.
- The act of writing in a dead friend's book is itself a small, loaded moment.

## Key Beats
- Marks the transition from Kael's notebook to the player's notebook.
- Introduces the ongoing journaling/recording mechanic.
- Should feel personal, not mechanical. The player is choosing to continue what Kael started.
