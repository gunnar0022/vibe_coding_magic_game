# Meeting the Wanderer

## Placement
- Late Act 1. After the brother's departure. The player is traveling toward the Crossroads or exploring the region.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Rahn

## What Happens
- The player encounters Rahn somewhere on the road or at a waypoint. He's already there — he doesn't appear, he's just present.
- First impression: calm where everyone else has been frantic. Quiet. Unbothered.
- He doesn't volunteer much. If the player engages, he shares small observations — about the land, about travel, about the way things grow differently now.
- He doesn't ask where the player is going or why. He doesn't seem to need to know.
- This is a brief encounter, not a deep conversation. Rahn is established as a recurring presence, not a companion.

## Key Beats
- Rahn should feel like a contrast to everything the player has experienced — the grief, the urgency, the village's desperation. He's none of those things.
- Plant the seed that this person has seen more than they're saying.
- No exposition about his backstory. Just presence. The depth comes later.
