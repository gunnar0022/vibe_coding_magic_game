# The Village Attack Aftermath

## Placement
- Immediately after the village attack. The dust settles.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Sael
- Senna
- Surviving villagers

## What Happens
- The immediate aftermath: bodies laid to rest, makeshift repairs beginning, the village in shock.
- Parents are dead. Kael is dead. About a quarter of the village is lost.
- The village functions on momentum, not emotion. People do what needs doing because stopping means feeling.
- Senna's father is dead. She steps into his role — not with confidence, with the nauseating feeling of filling shoes that don't fit.
- The brother is present but already changed. Something has locked into place inside him. He's holding it together outwardly but the trauma is pushing him toward departure.
- The player processes loss while the village processes survival.

## Key Beats
- The grief should be specific, not generic. These are named people the player spent time with.
- Senna's transition from "the chief's daughter" to "the person holding everything together" begins here.
- The brother's restlessness is visible but not yet articulated. He hasn't decided to leave yet — or hasn't admitted it.
- The notebook/spell book from Kael could be recovered here or shortly after.
