# Village Help — Senna's Appeal

## Placement
- After the brother's departure. The player is still in the village. This is Senna's pitch.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Senna

## What Happens
- With the brother gone, the village feels his absence. Another person who left.
- Senna approaches the player — not with a speech, but with honesty. She can't do this alone. She doesn't guilt the player. She tells the truth.
- The player can choose to invest time in rebuilding or begin moving outward. Either way, Senna's appeal is heard.
- If the player helps, early village rebuilding tasks begin — practical, grounding work that contrasts with the brother's restless departure.

## Key Beats
- Senna is not a guilt trip. Her appeal works because it's genuine.
- This is the community investment hook — the player's first real choice about where to put their energy.
- The brother's departure and Senna's appeal are two pulls in opposite directions. Neither is wrong.
