# First Spell

## Placement
- Early Act 1. After learning the magic field with Kael. The brother teaches the player their first spell.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Sael (the brother)

## What Happens
- Sael takes the player aside, evening, and teaches them their first spell.
- Introduces the mechanic of spell casting as a tutorial — focusing on a kanji image to channel mana.
- The player casts their first magic. Both siblings are surprised — it's unclear if the player has a natural affinity or if sharing magic is just easier than expected.
- The moment develops the unknown about magic. Neither sibling fully understands what just happened.
- This is a bonding moment — quiet, private, the brother sharing something he cares about with the person he cares about most.

## Key Beats
- The brother's enthusiasm for magic is genuine, not ominous yet. He's excited to share.
- The player's first cast should feel meaningful — a real moment of wonder.
- The ambiguity matters: is the player talented, or is this just how magic works? Nobody knows yet.
- Plants the seed for the brother's later trajectory — he's already pushing, already wanting more.
