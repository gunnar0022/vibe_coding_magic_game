# The Brother's Departure

## Locked Endpoints

- Occurs in the aftermath of the village attack.
- The brother cannot stay. The village is a wound he has to walk away from.
- He wants the player to come with him. Makes this clear with real emotional weight — shared pain, shared loss, "We lost the same people. Don't you want to make sure this never happens again?"
- This is not manipulation in the calculated sense. It's real love expressed through need for control, need for validation.
- He won't wait. He leaves.
- **The brother's departure — not the attack — is the true inciting incident.** The attack creates the conditions. His leaving creates the choice.

## What Follows

- The player is left in a damaged but living village.
- The world opens up. People with their own responses to what happened begin to emerge — Senna needs help rebuilding, Venn is out there researching, Rahn is somewhere on the road.
- The player's response to the brother's departure shapes the emotional trajectory, but the story moves forward through planned events and encounters, not a rigid path system.

## The Inciting Incident Scene

### Scene Context

**When:** Two days after the village attack. The immediate chaos has settled into numbness. Bodies have been laid to rest. Makeshift repairs are starting on damaged structures. The village is in shock — functioning on momentum, not emotion.

**Where:** The edge of the village, where the forest begins to the north. The brother has packed a small bag. He stands at the threshold between the community that has become unbearable and the wider world where he might find purpose.

**Who:** The player and the brother. Alone. Everyone else is occupied with immediate recovery work.
