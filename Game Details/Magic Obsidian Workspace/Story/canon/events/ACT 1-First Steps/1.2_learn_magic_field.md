

# THE CREEK SCENE — Kael and the Player

## The Scene That Shows Who They Are

**Setting:** A rocky outcrop just outside the village, overlooking the creek valley. The water moves fast here, tumbling over stones. The vegetation along the banks is thick — unnervingly so, the way everything grows now. Mid-afternoon. Warm.

**Context:** The village doesn't understand magic. Not really, this is a small farming community, not a center of learning. A few people have gotten a couple of symbols to work — enough to be useful, enough to be a little frightening. Nobody can explain why it works. Most people don't try. Some mutter about gods. Some think it's poison. The brother has been practicing obsessively, convinced it's a tool they need to survive. Their mother uses a simple water-cleansing symbol she picked up from a passing trader, and treats it the way she'd treat any new chore — carefully, without thinking too hard about it.

## Design Notes

**The mana bar is Kael's gift.** 

**Kael discovered the mana field through temperament, not intelligence.** 

**The village's relationship to magic should feel primitive.** 

**The brother is absent from this scene.**
