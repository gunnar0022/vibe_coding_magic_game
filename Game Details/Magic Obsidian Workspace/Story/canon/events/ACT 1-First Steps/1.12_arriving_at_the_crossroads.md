# Arriving at the Crossroads

## Placement
- Late Act 1. The player reaches the Crossroads — the regional hub where multiple communities intersect.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Crossroads residents and traders
- Possibly Senna or a village contact who pointed the player here

## What Happens
- The player arrives at the Crossroads — a larger, more diverse settlement than the home village. Multiple communities trade and interact here.
- The scale shift is important. The home village was small and insular. The Crossroads has people from different backgrounds with different beliefs about magic, different survival strategies, different attitudes toward strangers.
- This is where the world opens up mechanically — new quests, new NPCs, new areas accessible from this hub.
- The player hears rumors, news, and conflicting information about the wider world.

## Key Beats
- The Crossroads should feel alive and slightly overwhelming after the quiet village.
- Different people here have different superstitions about magic — the first taste of the competing frameworks the player will encounter throughout the game.
- The player is a newcomer, not a hero. Nobody knows them here. They have to earn trust.
