# See the Village

## Placement
- Opening of the game. The player's first experience of the world.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- The brother (Sael)
- Parents (Thorne, Elsa)
- Childhood friend (Kael)
- Senna and her father
- Village residents

## What Happens
- The player experiences daily life in a small, stable post-Fall village.
- Introduces the family: Thorne (builder), Elsa (practical magic user, water-cleansing symbol), Sael (older brother, competent, warm).
- Introduces Kael (childhood friend, sensitive, observant).
- The village is functional — farming, building, communal work. Stable for decades since the collapse.
- Magic exists at the edges — a few people have gotten symbols to work, most treat it cautiously. Not a center of learning.
- Establishes what the player stands to lose before it's taken.

## Key Beats
- The village should feel lived-in, not idyllic. Real community with real work.
- Sael should be visibly competent and warm — the player's role model before everything changes.
- The brother's interest in magic is already present — he's been practicing obsessively, sees it as a survival tool.
- The tone is calm but not safe. The world outside is dangerous. The village is a pocket of stability.
