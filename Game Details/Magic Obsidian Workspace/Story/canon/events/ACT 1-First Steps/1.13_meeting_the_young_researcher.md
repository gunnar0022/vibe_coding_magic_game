# Meeting the Young Researcher

## Placement
- Late Act 1. At or near the Crossroads. One of the first major NPCs the player meets after leaving the village region.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Venn

## What Happens
- The player meets Venn — Eloth's student, early twenties, restless and quick-talking.
- A key point: Venn notices the spell book the player is carrying from their fallen friend (Kael). She marks the player as a fellow scholar and runs with her gut sense. She's very extroverted and a bit impatient.
- Venn is excited to meet someone who's actually recording observations. Most people she encounters treat magic with fear or superstition — the player is carrying a notebook full of real data.
- She introduces the idea of Eloth and the research camp, the collaborative work they're doing, and invites the player into it.

## Key Beats
- Venn's energy should contrast with everything the player has been through. She's enthusiastic where the player is grieving. She makes the world feel fascinating instead of threatening.
- The notebook is the bridge — Kael's legacy connects the player to Venn's world of inquiry.
- This is the hook for the research storyline that runs through Acts 2 and 3.
- Venn doesn't know what the player has lost. Her excitement might feel insensitive, and that's okay — it's genuine, not callous.
