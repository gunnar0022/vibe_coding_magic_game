# The Village Attack

## Locked Endpoints

- Coordinated assault by altered humanoid creatures — hollowed humans, though the player doesn't know this yet.
- The attackers came from a nearby settlement that failed. Ran out of resources. Kept taking from themselves. Hollowed out. Simple desperation produced simple, bestial forms.
- The player's parents are killed.
- The player's childhood friend is killed.
- About a quarter of the village is lost — lives, structures, resources.
- **The brother saves the player's life.** A clear, visceral moment. This is the foundation everything else rests on.
- The village survives. Wounded, not destroyed. Rebuilding has weight because there's actually something left to save.

## Narrative Function

- The attackers are the endpoint of the brother's potential arc. The player sees the destination before the journey begins.
- Finding the origin settlement later is optional environmental storytelling, not a major questline.
- The attack creates the conditions. The brother's departure creates the choice.

## The Player's Perspective

### The Moment Before

The player and brother are at the forest edge, north of the village. They've been collecting wood for repair work—the fallen trees from the winter storms are finally dry enough to work with. The brother is showing the player how to judge which pieces are strong enough, how to see the grain and predict where they'll split.
