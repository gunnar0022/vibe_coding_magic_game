# More Mechanical Tutorial

## Placement
- Early Act 1. After first spell. Continued gameplay onboarding before the attack.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Sael and/or other village members

## What Happens
- Continued mechanical introduction — combat basics, environmental interaction, resource gathering, or expanded spell use.
- The player gets comfortable with the game's systems in the safe context of the village.
- The village's daily rhythm continues around the player. Normal life. Routine.
- This normalcy is the setup for the attack — the player needs to feel settled before everything is torn apart.

## Key Beats
- Functional, not narrative-heavy. The story has been seeded; this is gameplay foundation.
- The village should still feel alive and populated during tutorial segments.
- Any further magic use reinforces the basics: draw from the environment, visualize the kanji, the world gives freely when it has something to give.
