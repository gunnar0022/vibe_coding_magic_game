# Meeting Another Community

## Placement
- Mid Act 3. Flexible — can land anywhere in the middle stretch. Widens the world one more time before the endgame narrows it back to the personal.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- A new settlement with its own culture, superstitions, and relationship with magic
- Possibly a companion

## What Happens
- The player encounters a community they haven't visited before. Different from the Crossroads, different from the home village, different from the Enclave's theology.
- This community has its own approach to magic — its own superstitions, its own casting rituals, its own beliefs about what's safe and what's dangerous. All of it roughly one year old, like everyone else's.
- The specifics are flexible. Options include:
  - A community that's handling magic well — cautious, sustainable, respectful of limits. Contrast with Sael.
  - A community teetering on the edge — using too much, starting to see early signs of hollowing, but hasn't collapsed yet. Mirror of Sael.
  - A community that has rejected magic entirely — saw what it did to neighbors and banned all casting. Fear as a valid survival strategy.
- The player brings their accumulated knowledge into the encounter. They know more about magic than this community does. The question is what they do with that.

## Key Beats
- Reinforces the superstition snippet: every community is one year into this and guessing differently.
- The player's reaction to this community reflects what they've learned and what they believe.
- This is worldbuilding that serves character: how the player engages with a new community tells them (and us) who they're becoming.
- Could plant seeds for future content or side quests without needing resolution in this act.
