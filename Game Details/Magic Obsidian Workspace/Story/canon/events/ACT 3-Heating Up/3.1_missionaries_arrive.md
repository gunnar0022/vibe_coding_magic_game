# The Missionaries Arrive at the Crossroads

## Placement
- Early Act 3. The player has met Venn and Eloth, has a baseline understanding of magic-as-system. Now encountering magic-as-theology cracks the world open at the right moment.
- The missionaries' warning about complex corrupted beings in the north lands early in Act 3, then the player discovers the feral attackers' origin later and realizes hollowing is mechanical, not theological. The two pieces create productive friction.

## Scene Goals
- Crack the world open.
- Introduce the Holy Enclave as a society large enough to *send* people outward. This implies organization, surplus, and confidence the Crossroads communities don't have.
- Present a completely different framework for understanding magic 
- Deliver the first warning about the Unholy Kingdom 


## What Happens
- Five missionaries arrive at the Crossroads from the far north.
- They introduce themselves as servants of Her and explain their purpose: spreading Her word, warning of the Damned to the north, recruiting the faithful.
- They demonstrate casting framed as prayer 
- They share news of the wider world
- The Crossroads communities react with a mix of fascination, skepticism, and alarm. 

## Key Beats
- The missionaries are genuinely impressive. Combat-trained, coordinated, carrying standardized spells. 
- They're also genuinely sincere.
- They speak of **miracles** 
- The warning about the Unholy Kingdom is the most important content they deliver
- The Holy Enclave is holding them off but needs help.
- Venn would be fascinated by the theological framework and especially the miracle claims.
