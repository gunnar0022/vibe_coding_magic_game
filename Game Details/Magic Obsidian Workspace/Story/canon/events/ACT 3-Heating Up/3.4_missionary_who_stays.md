# The Missionary Who Stays

## Placement
- Early-to-mid Act 3. After the other four missionaries have departed south. This is the quieter, personal aftermath of the faith crisis — not a dramatic moment but a lingering one.

## Scene Goals
- Give the player a recurring NPC who embodies the cost of losing a worldview. 
- Create a character who can serve as a bridge between the Enclave's theology and Eloth's analytical approach 
- Reinforce the game's theme that certainty is dangerous and uncertainty is honest.

## Why This One Breaks
- Not simply from seeing non-believers cast. All five missionaries see that. Its meeting **Eloth**.
- Eloth explains magic systematically — mana pools, kanji as funnels shaped by meaning, the hollowing as mechanical consequence of self-draw. His framework is more internally consistent than the theology. 
- The missionary recognizes that Eloth's model is *better*. 
- The killing blow is the miracles. This person has *felt* miracles — the communal prayer that strengthens fighters, that sustains the Enclave against the Damned. That experience was the bedrock of their faith. Eloth doesn't understand the miracles, and allows this character to suspend disbelief in Her and sit in-between, wondering if its just because there's a missing piece that would explain that too. 
- The missionary can't go back and preach something they no longer believe. 

## What Happens
- The other four missionaries depart south, continuing their mission.
- They gravitate toward Eloth's camp or the Crossroads. 
- Over time, they become a fixture — someone the player can talk to about faith, doubt, the nature of magic, and the question of whether understanding something is the same as taking away its meaning.

## Key Beats
- This person is not a comedy figure 
- They carry useful knowledge: the Enclave's "sacred battle prayers" (standardized combat kanji)
- They mirror the player's experience. The player has been accumulating contradictory information about magic from every community.
- They don't hate the other four for continuing.
