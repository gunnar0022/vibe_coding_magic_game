# Encountering a Complex Corrupted Being

## Placement
- Mid-to-late Act 3. After the player has seen Sael slipping and received their own corruption warning. The context makes this encounter hit differently.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- A corrupted being that can still think, speak, and reason
- Possibly a companion (Venn, Rahn, or Sael)

## What Happens
- The player encounters a corrupted being that is not feral. It talks. It reasons. It has a twisted version of the personality it used to have.
- This is the corruption spectrum made visceral. The feral attackers from Act 1 were simple desperation hollowed into beasts. This one had complex motives — grief, ambition, love, pride — and the hollowing amplified all of them into something monstrous but intelligent.
- Origin is flexible: could be a Kingdom scout or refugee who escaped the subjugation cascade, could be entirely local — someone who hollowed independently from their own choices.
- The encounter may not be pure combat. The being might want something. Might offer information. Might try to justify what it's become.

## Key Beats
- The player is forced to confront what Sael could become. Not feral — something worse. Something that can still talk to you while being fundamentally broken.
- This is the first time the player sees the "intelligent" end of the corruption spectrum in person.
- Whatever companion is present reacts according to their worldview: Venn is horrified but fascinated, Rahn sees the pattern repeating, Sael might see power.
