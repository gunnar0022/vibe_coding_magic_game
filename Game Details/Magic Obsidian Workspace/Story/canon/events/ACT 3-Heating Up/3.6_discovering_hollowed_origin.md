# Discovering the Hollowed Origin

## Placement
- Mid Act 3. Can land before or after the brother_slipping event — either order works, but both together create the full picture.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Possibly Venn (would want to document everything)
- The failed settlement itself (environmental storytelling)

## What Happens
- The player finds the origin of the Act 1 attackers — the failed settlement that hollowed itself.
- This is not a quest marker. It's optional environmental storytelling. The player stumbles across it or follows clues.
- The settlement tells its own story through what's left: abandoned homes, journals, signs of rationing, signs of desperation, signs of the final transformation. The progression is legible in the ruins.
- The journals track a community that ran out of resources, kept casting to survive, started drawing from themselves, and never stopped. Simple desperation. Simple hollowing. Simple feral creatures at the end.
- The player already knows what hollowing is mechanically. This isn't a reveal — it's an emotional gut punch. These were people. Real people who made the same choice Sael is making right now.

## Key Beats
- The settlement should feel like a cautionary mirror, not a dungeon.
- The journals matter. Voices of people who didn't know what was happening to them.
- Venn, if present, would be quiet for once. This is the cost of the knowledge she's been chasing.
- The distance between this settlement and Sael's trajectory should be obvious without anyone saying it out loud.
