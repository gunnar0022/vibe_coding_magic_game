# The Deep Wild Encounter

## Placement
- Mid-to-late Act 3. Flexible placement — works as a breather between heavier story beats, or as a late-Act moment of perspective before the endgame closes in.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- The environment itself (high-mana old growth region)
- Possibly a companion

## What Happens
- The player reaches an area of extreme ambient mana. Old growth forest, untouched by human activity, dense with life. The mana here is overwhelming.
- Wild amplification is visible everywhere — plants, animals, the air itself hums with energy. Resonances are audible or palpable. The world at its most alive and most dangerous.
- The experience is awe, not combat. The player gets a glimpse of what magic *is* when humans aren't fighting over it or draining it. The world giving freely, abundantly, without cost.
- This is also mechanically interesting: in an area this mana-rich, the player could cast freely without ever needing to self-draw. The temptation and the beauty exist simultaneously.
- The wildlife here is amplified but not corrupted — because nothing has been drained. This is what the natural state looks like.

## Key Beats
- A beauty beat. The world is worth something beyond what humans take from it.
- Contrasts sharply with the mana dead zones, the failed settlement, the corrupted encounters. This is the other end of the spectrum.
- Raises the stakes for everything: this is what's at risk if hollowing spreads unchecked.
- The player's relationship with magic should feel different here — generous instead of transactional.
