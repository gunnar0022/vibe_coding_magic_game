# The Collaborative Breakthrough

## Placement
- Late Act 3. After the player has accumulated enough experience and data through the mid-Act events. This is the hope beat before the endgame.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Eloth
- Venn
- The body of research they've built together throughout the game

## What Happens
- The collaborative research project reaches a critical milestone. The accumulated observations, field data, and experiments converge into a genuine breakthrough.
- What the breakthrough is exactly is flexible, but it should relate to hollowing — either understanding the mechanism well enough to articulate exactly what's happening to Sael, or discovering that early-stage hollowing might be slowed or reversed under specific conditions.
- This is the payoff for every research mission, every data point, every careful observation the player contributed.
- The breakthrough also produces dangerous knowledge — the same understanding that could save someone could be weaponized. This is Eloth's nightmare: the thing he's been cautious about all along.

## Key Beats
- The player should feel like they *earned* this. Their work with Eloth and Venn wasn't busywork — it led somewhere real.
- Eloth is cautiously hopeful but immediately aware of the implications. This knowledge needs to be held carefully.
- Venn is excited and wants to push further. She sees possibility where Eloth sees risk.
- This sets up the Act 4 mentor confrontation: this knowledge is what a corrupted player/brother might try to take by force.
- The breakthrough should feel provisional — promising but untested. Not a cure. A direction.
