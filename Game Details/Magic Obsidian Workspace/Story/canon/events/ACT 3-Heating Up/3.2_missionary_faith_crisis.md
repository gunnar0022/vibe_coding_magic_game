# The Missionary Faith Crisis

## Placement
- Immediately follows or overlaps with X_missionaries_arrive. Part of the same visit, not a separate event — the crisis happens *during* their stay at the Crossroads, not days later.

## Scene Goals
- Show what happens when a deeply held theological framework collides with evidence that contradicts it. This is the Superstition snippet playing out live.
- Create genuine tension that isn't combat-based. Five devout people watching non-believers use "Her gift" without prayer, without reverence, and getting the same results.
- The player witnesses this and must decide what they think — about the theology, about the magic, about certainty itself.

## What Triggers the Crisis
- The missionaries see Crossroads residents casting magic with no prayer, no ritual, no theological framework. Just visualization and intent. The effects are identical to what the missionaries produce through worship.
- This directly contradicts their core belief that magic is a relationship with Her. If non-believers can channel Her gift without acknowledging Her, then either She doesn't require faith (which undermines the entire theology) or this isn't Her gift at all (which undermines their identity).

## How the Five React
- **Most hold firm.** 
- **One breaks.** 
- **Internal friction.** 

## Key Beats
- The missionaries don't become enemies or hostile. Their crisis is internal and interpersonal, not directed at the Crossroads communities.
- The player gets to see faith tested from the outside. This mirrors the player's own experience of accumulating contradictory information about magic from every community they visit.
- Venn would find this fascinating and possibly insensitive in her excitement ("But don't you see? If it works without prayer, that means the system is *independent* of belief, which is actually more interesting—"). Eloth would be more careful, recognizing that dismantling someone's worldview has consequences.
