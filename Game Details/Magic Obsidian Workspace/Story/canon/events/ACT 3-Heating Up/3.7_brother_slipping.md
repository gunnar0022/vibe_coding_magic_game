# The Brother Is Slipping

## Placement
- Mid Act 3. After the missionary sequence. The player now has enough understanding of hollowing to recognize what they're seeing.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Sael
- Possibly Venn or Eloth (if the player brings observations back to them)

## What Happens
- The player encounters Sael again. He's changed since the last reunion.
- Still articulate, still warm in flashes — but the edges are wrong.
- His protectiveness has curdled into something possessive. His strength is the only thing he values about himself now.
- He's pushing harder than ever. Fighting things he doesn't need to fight. Casting in drained areas without hesitation.
- The player can see the hollowing pattern playing out in real time — the same pattern they've been studying with Eloth.
- Sael doesn't think anything is wrong. He thinks he's getting stronger. He's not wrong — he is. That's the problem.
- The player has to decide how to respond: confront him, observe silently, report to Eloth, or try to intervene directly.

## Key Beats
- The horror is recognition, not surprise. The player knows exactly what this trajectory looks like.
- Sael is still *him* enough that the player can't write him off. He still cares. He's still the brother who saved their life.
- The distance between "the brother" and "the feral attackers from Act 1" is suddenly, visibly measurable.
