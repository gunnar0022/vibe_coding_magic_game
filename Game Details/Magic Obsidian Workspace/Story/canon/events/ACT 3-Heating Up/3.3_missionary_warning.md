# The Warning of the Unholy Kingdom

## Placement
- Part of the missionary visit sequence. Can be delivered during the initial arrival or in a follow-up conversation once the missionaries have settled in at the Crossroads.

## Scene Goals
- Deliver the first concrete intelligence about a large-scale, organized corrupted threat
- Plant the seed that complex corruption 
- Give the player real, actionable information buried inside theological framing. 
- Establish that the world beyond the Crossroads is bigger, more dangerous, and more complex than the player imagined.

## What the Missionaries Know (Firsthand)
- Their northern neighbors used to be friendly, prosperous
- Contact broke down rapidly after magic appeared, within months.
- Traders stopped coming back.
- Gatherers were attacked by people who were physically changed but organized and armed.
- The changed people are not feral. They coordinate. They use weapons and magic. 
- There have been border skirmishes. The Enclave holds its ground but can't push forward.
- The situation is a standstill and the Enclave needs allies.

## What the Missionaries Believe (Theological Interpretation)
- The neighbors rejected Her word and were punished with transformation.
- The corruption is divine judgment on the greedy and faithless.
- Anyone who uses magic without prayer risks the same fate.
- The Enclave's faith is what protects them from the same destiny.
- The Damned are a warning to all humanity about the cost of hubris.

## What the Missionaries Don't Know
- The mechanical cause of hollowing
- That their own restraint-through-worship is what actually protects them, not divine favor.
- The scale of the Kingdom's internal experiments (human experimentation, engineered hollowing).
- That the Kingdom is deliberately waiting for a breakthrough, not stuck in a stalemate.
- That hollowed servants — a new category of corruption — exist within the Kingdom.

## Key Beats
- The warning should feel genuinely alarming- not fairy tales
- The player can extract useful tactical intelligence if they listen carefully
- Eloth or Venn would want to ask detailed follow-up questions 
- The brother (if present or if this reaches him) would be intensely interested.
