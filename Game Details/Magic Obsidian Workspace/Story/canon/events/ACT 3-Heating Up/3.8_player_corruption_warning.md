# The Player's Own Corruption Warning

## Placement
- Mid Act 3. Around the same time as or shortly after seeing Sael slipping. The one-two punch: watching it happen to him, then realizing it's happening to you.

## Who's Involved
- The player
- Eloth or Venn (whoever notices)

## What Happens
- The player receives a direct signal that they are not immune to hollowing.
- Could be Eloth noticing something in the player's casting patterns, Venn flagging an anomaly in their shared research data, or the player experiencing a moment where casting feels different — easier, hungrier, less costly than it should.
- This isn't a punishment. It's proportional to how much the player has been self-drawing throughout the game. A player who's been cautious gets a mild warning. A player who's been reckless gets a serious one.
- The reframe: the player has been watching Sael hollow and thinking it's *his* problem. Now it's theirs too.

## Key Beats
- This should feel personal, not mechanical. Not a UI popup — a character moment.
- Eloth's reaction matters. He's seen this before. His caution suddenly makes visceral sense.
- The player now has to apply everything they've learned about restraint to themselves, not just observe it in others.
