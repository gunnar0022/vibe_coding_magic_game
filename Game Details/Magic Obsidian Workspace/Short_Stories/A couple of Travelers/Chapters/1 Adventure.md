With a stretch and a yawn, Vard opened his eyes in the shimmering light from the above canopy. Instinctively, he patted his chest where a bound leather tome lay, making sure it hadn't run off while he slept. Without thinking, he picked it up and started flipping through pages and skimming the sparse contents within. Contained in the book were various mysterious symbols, and Vard's notes on such symbols. Magic, some might choose to call it. No one's really can say for sure, try as hard as they might. After all, it's hard to be sure about something you didn't know existed the previous year. 
After a minute of paging through his tome, he noticed his companion, Thorm, begin to rise. 
Quietly and deliberately, Thorm grabbed some of the nearby sticks they had prepared and arranged them in a neat stack. Then, held out his hand towards the stack and closed his eyes gently. A lick of flame was born. It grew forwards towards the sticks, enveloping and igniting them. In just a few seconds, they had a very reasonable fire going.
"You're getting pretty good at that," Vard said, partially teasingly. 
"Considering how many morning's it's been, I'd hope so," Thorm responded. Vard moved to join him by the flame. It wasn't a cold morning by any means, but the comfort of the fire was still welcome. Still waking up, Thorm moved with a smooth motion for their travel sack and retrieved a couple of large, berry-shaped fruits. Each around the size of two fists pressed together by the palm. 
"I don't want to sound ungrateful, but I'd love to have something other than melonberries for once," Vard grumbled. 
"If it weren't for these melonberries, we'd likely be stuck working on the farm every day." Thorm pointed out. 
"But don't you feel weird eating something that's only been around for a few decades? I mean, sure, that's longer than we've been around, but I heard these things used to only be about as big as the end of your thumb!" Vard stated with a grin. 
"You say that, but aren't you also the person who volunteered to explore and expand what we understand about this new magic stuff?" Thorm poked back, beginning to smirk himself. 
"I can't believe we were the only ones! How could you not want to know more about-- " he began as he spread his palms upwards, and let a few flames jump playfully into the world. "This," he finished. Vard had learned the symbol of fire through resonance. It came directly from... He wasn't sure where from, but it didn't come from another person. One day he was making a fire for his family, going through the motions. The next moment, burning in his mind's eye was a strange symbol. Since then, he'd been able to use *fire* a bit more freely than those he shared it with. 
Between bites of his roasted melonberry, Vard asked, "Do you think we'll find any today?"
"If we're lucky. Although, we haven't seemed to have much of that lately. It must be around two weeks since the last one," Thorm said wistfully. The memory from two weeks ago was still clear in their minds. They had stumbled in a clearing in the woods with the greatest stone they had ever seen. Upon examination, they found an unnaturally smooth engraving of a symbol inlaid on it. 
Thorm had opened his own journal to the page with notes about the stone symbol they found. The air in front of Thorm's once again extended hand moved with a gentle blue glowing swirl before taking the form of an ordinary stone, and falling to the ground. 
"Doesn't seem all that useful on its own," Vard said somewhat wryly. 
"But if that was really a manifestation on that stone, then we've already made a huge discovery. Not to mention the untested pairings to go with *stone*," Thorm said getting a little worked up. 
Vard peered over at his companion's journal to see the symbol again.
"At least it doesn't look too complicated," he remarked. Still looking at the journal, he began forming his own magic stone, this time catching it before it fell. "It's a pretty good size and weight too," he said giving it a little toss in the air, catching it as it fell. Then, he took aim a tree's trunk across small clearing they had camped out in, and launched it. Hit.
After a couple moments of hesitation, Thorm produced another stone for himself, and threw it at the tree's trunk. Another hit. However, this was no great feat. Not even 8 adults would have been able to encompass such a tree with their arms outstretched. 
"We should get moving," Thorm said while getting up off the ground. From his extended hand, this time water poured down and dowsed the fire. His first resonate magic. 

Before long they were on their feet, moving through the woods.
